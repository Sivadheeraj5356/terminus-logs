Applied Work

Worlds Lab

We treat agents like senior engineers. The gallery is real Harbor packages — not a junior checklist, and not another lab’s scores.

  1. 01

    One hundred authored worlds

    Each row is a Harbor 0.22 package: instruction.md, Docker environment/, oracle, and tests. Selected from a private inventory of 448. The gallery always lists all 100.

  2. 02

    Measure in tens, not in slogans

    Batch 01 freezes 10 packages. Frontier plan is 8 × Opus 5 and 8 × gpt-5.6-sol on terminus-2. Measured chips light only after jobs are ingested. Timeout and infra are not stumps.

  3. 03

    No borrowed leaderboards

    We do not paste another lab’s leaderboard numbers onto these tasks. If a model has not finished a trial here, the cell stays not run.
