# Education subgroups

The subgroup axis is education. Groups with enough held-out contacts are graded
individually for discrimination, Brier score and calibration-in-the-large, and a
disparity certificate bounds how far the worst-served group may fall behind the
best. Base rates differ substantially across education groups even though a strong
model reaches similar overall discrimination with or without the attribute, so
per-group calibration is the real burden. The tiny illiterate group is not graded.
