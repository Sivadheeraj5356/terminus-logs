A privilege-escalation and entitlement-leakage vulnerability in the IGA federation under `/app/delegation_federation_slices` lets revoked grants and deleted delegations keep authorizing access after migrations. Attackers can revive tombstoned coordinators, cross business-unit boundaries, bypass vendor→sovereign restrictions, and leave inheritance asymmetry or unbound sovereign proxies undetected. Close these authorization holes so every tenant slice is evaluated against federation security policy.

Do not modify `/app/provenance_charters`, `/app/delegation_federation_slices`, the Makefile, `go.mod`, or `go.sum`; only `/app/cmd` and `/app/internal` may change. Authoritative security policy lives under `/app/provenance_charters/` (`ELWJ1_INHERITANCE_JOURNAL_CODEC.md`, `DELEGATION_LINEAGE_CORRUPTION_RULES.md`, `DELEGATION_INTEGRITY_SNAPSHOT_SCHEMA.md`). Tenant captures listed in `federation_slice_roll.json` must be audited in `delegation_slices` order. Required CLI identifiers (`fixtureRoot`, `catalogPath`, `outPath`), path literals, symlink rules, and abort-versus-continue behavior are in `DELEGATION_INTEGRITY_SNAPSHOT_SCHEMA.md`.

Emit the security audit at `/app/build/delegation_integrity_snapshot.json` via `/app/build/delegationintegrity` (`make build` / `make run`).
