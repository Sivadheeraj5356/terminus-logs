Operate the local host firewall policy controller in `/app`. It does not converge after an interrupted apply, conflicting configuration drop-ins, or a restart with active runtime overrides. Operators rely on `nxctl apply` and `nxctl recover` to reconcile durable controller state with the effective host policy and ownership model documented under `/app/docs`.

Administrative convergence must satisfy layered configuration precedence, counter lineage across chain regeneration, crash recovery after partial staging, preservation of foreign operator rules, validation that rejects invalid effective policy without mutating durable state, accurate `nxctl status --json` reporting, and upgrade of supported legacy ownership manifests. The controller must regenerate backend, manifest, and audit state through the normal apply and recover workflow; direct edits to durable state files or verifier-only artifacts are insufficient.

Durable state lives at `/app/state/backend.json`, `/app/state/ownership-manifest.json`, `/app/state/runtime-overrides.json`, and `/app/state/audit.jsonl`. The `nxctl status --json` command reports `schema_version`, `phase`, `generation`, `pending_generation`, `managed_rules`, `foreign_rules`, and `reason_codes`. Audit lines record `operation`, `phase`, `outcome`, and `reason_code`. Contract details for CLI invocation, status semantics, drop-in precedence and runtime-override limits, crash barriers, and reconciliation semantics are in `/app/docs/cli-contract.md`, `/app/docs/configuration-precedence.md`, and `/app/docs/reconciliation-contract.md`. Recover on converged state must be idempotent with respect to generation. Use `/app/environment/bin/nxctl` with the conventions from those docs. Verifier helpers `ref_model`, `backend_factory`, and `fixture_generator` are not part of the operator workflow.
