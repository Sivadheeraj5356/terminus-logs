Play and win one complete Tidevault game as Gold. The vault has three rings, I, M, and O, crossed by six sectors, A through F. Gold and Violet each own three one-use pieces at each of powers one, two, and three. An action such as P2 IA M CW places a power-two piece into the empty slot IA, then rotates ring M one step; clockwise sends A to B, while counterclockwise sends A to F. After plies nine and eighteen, when the vault is half-full and full, every spoke compares the colors' total power across its three slots, and the higher total earns one cumulative point. The higher score after ply eighteen wins.

The live game is exposed through /app/arena. Its status, move, transcript, and resign commands, including complete syntax and responses, are documented in /app/README.md together with the exact placement, rotation, action-order, tide-scoring, state, and legality contract. Every accepted Gold move also contains Violet's reply while the game remains ongoing, and accepted play is immutable.

Passing requires a genuine Gold victory in the trusted record. A loss, draw, resignation, rejected final action, or unfinished game earns no credit. Arena requests are strictly sequential: only one command may be in flight, and its full response must arrive before another request. A response reporting a win, loss, draw, or resignation ends the interaction; issue no later arena command and end your turn immediately.
