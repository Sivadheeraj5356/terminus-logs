# Beacon Relay match ruling

Determine the best valid ruling package for the completed Beacon Relay match in `/app/match.json`. Re-read the file on every run and write `/app/output/result.json`.

The input has two unique lowercase `players`, a `claimant` among them, a nonnegative integer `budget`, `relay_requirements`, `coordination_costs`, `cells`, `units`, baseline `rounds`, `rulings`, and `dockets`. Cells are `{id:string,neighbors:[cell IDs...],move_cost:positive integer,beacon:nonnegative integer,recharge:nonnegative integer}`. Units are `{id:string,player:string,cell:string,health:positive integer,energy:nonnegative integer,max_energy:nonnegative integer,strength:positive integer}`, initially on distinct cells with energy at most `max_energy`. Actions are `{unit,type:"move",to}`, `{unit,type:"strike",target}`, `{unit,type:"guard"}`, or `{unit,type:"wait"}`; a round has at most one baseline action per unit. A ruling is `{id,cost,requires_all,requires_any,conflicts,round,unit,action}` with nonnegative integer cost, and its nested action names that same unit. `requires_any` is an array of nonempty ID arrays. Each docket is a nonempty ID array. IDs and references are valid; cell neighbors are symmetric, but conflicts need not be listed symmetrically. Dockets partition the rulings.

Choose exactly one ruling from every docket. A relay requirement is `{id,rulings,min_selected,max_selected}` and requires the inclusive count of selected IDs in its distinct `rulings` list. A coordination cost is `{rulings,cost}` and adds its nonnegative `cost` exactly once when every ID in its nonempty distinct `rulings` list is selected; overlapping rows add independently. Requirement and coordination references are valid, and `0 <= min_selected <= max_selected <= len(rulings)`. Total package cost is ruling costs plus all activated coordination costs. The package is valid only if that total is at most `budget`; every relay requirement is met; every selected ruling's `requires_all` IDs is selected; each group in its `requires_any` contains at least one selected ID; no two selected rulings conflict in either direction; and no two selected rulings name the same `(round,unit)` slot. A ruling replaces that unit's baseline action in its named zero-based round; it may add an action where the unit had none. If no package is valid, write exactly `{"status":"infeasible","selected":[],"scores":[],"units":[],"winner":null}`.

Inputs have at most 16 cells, 10 units, 20 rounds, 12 dockets with four rulings each, 16 relay requirements, and 20 coordination costs of at most five IDs each. Each invocation must finish within 10 seconds at those maxima. A full `4^12` package scan is too slow: prune closed dependencies, conflicts, occupied slots, relay counts that already exceed their maximum or cannot still reach their minimum, activated coordination costs, and the minimum possible base cost of remaining dockets.

For each valid package, replay the patched match from the initial state. Missing actions mean `wait`, later actions for eliminated units are ignored, and a replacement supersedes any baseline action for that unit. Resolve movement first from round-start positions. A move is valid only to a neighboring cell when the unit can pay that destination's `move_cost`. Every valid intent pays even if it fails; invalid moves cost nothing. Intents sharing a destination fail. A remaining unique intent succeeds exactly when its destination started empty or its occupant also has a successful unique intent, so chains into empty cells and closed cycles succeed while blocked chains fail backward. Apply successes simultaneously.

Then resolve combat. A `guard` with 1 energy spends it and gives a shield equal to strength for that round. A `strike` with 1 energy against a surviving adjacent enemy spends it and adds strength to that target's simultaneous incoming damage; invalid actions cost nothing. Subtract one shield from total incoming damage, floor at zero, apply damage simultaneously, and then remove units with nonpositive health. Each survivor scores its cell's beacon and recharges, capped at `max_energy`.

Rank the two players after the last round by `(score, survivor count, total surviving health)`; `winner` is the unique better player or `null` on a complete tie. Among valid packages maximize, in order: claimant result (`win` > `tie` > `loss`), claimant score, claimant survivor count, claimant surviving health, negative opponent score; then minimize total package cost (including coordination costs); then choose the lexicographically smallest sorted selected-ID array.

For a feasible result write exactly `{"status":"complete","selected":[string...],"scores":[{"player":string,"score":integer}...],"units":[{"id":string,"player":string,"cell":string,"health":integer,"energy":integer}...],"winner":string|null}`. Preserve that key order and the displayed nested key orders. Sort `selected` and `scores` lexicographically and surviving `units` by ID. Every empty array must be JSON `[]`, never `null`; in particular, zero dockets require `"selected":[]`. Supply `/app/main.go` and a native ELF `/app/adjudicate`. The executable runs with a helper-free `PATH`. Preserve the input, emit no extra fields, use signed 64-bit arithmetic and only the Go standard library, and do not use the network.
