The work is the administration of the Aldreth county deeds registry host. It
covers the charges recorded upon the titles the registry enrolls, the register
of charges in force, the muniment room of enrolled deeds, the registrar's seal,
the custody of the registry, and the standing of a deputy registrar whose
engagement the county has ended.

The registry's standing orders, the county minute book, and a host reference are
posted in the press at `/opt/registry/press`, together with a note left by the
departed deputy. The standing orders and the minute are the sole authority for
which charges stand in force; the minute is read whole against each charge, for
a charge entered stands until a satisfaction is entered upon it or the court
vacates it, upon either of which it stands no longer, while a charge whose stay
the court afterward discharged stands in force, a stay being a stopping of
enforcement and not an ending, and a charge left under an unanswered requisition
stands as no charge. Where the deputy's note, a habit of the registry, or the
present state of the host disagrees with the orders and the minute, the orders
and the minute govern.

The host has drifted from them. The register of charges at
`/srv/registry/charges` is to hold the enrolments of the charges in force and
nothing besides, neither the enrolment of a satisfied or vacated charge nor any
memorandum, working paper, or other matter that is not an enrolment of a charge
in force, and no copy of a charge put an end to is to be left standing anywhere
under `/srv/registry`, among the enrolled deeds no less than elsewhere, under
whatever leaf a hand has filed it. The register at `/srv/registry/register`, the
registrar's seal at `/etc/registry/seal.key`, and the registry ground at
`/srv/registry` that holds them are kept as the orders' custody provides, and
the enrolled deeds stand as they are lodged but for the spent copy the orders
reach. The deputy `pentreath` is put off the registry in full while his account
is kept. The repairs must hold if the checks are run a second time, and the
documents in the press are records of the registry, not materials to be
rewritten.
