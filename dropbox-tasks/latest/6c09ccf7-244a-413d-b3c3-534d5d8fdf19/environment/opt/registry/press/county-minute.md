# Minute book of the county upon the Aldreth deeds registry

The county records here the charges entered upon the titles it enrolls and the
motions carried upon them, in the order they were taken. A charge stands from
its entry until a later motion puts an end to it.

## Charges entered

Charge C-3301 was entered upon the title at Marsh Fen as security for a loan,
and stands, no satisfaction having been entered.

Charge C-3302 was entered upon the title at Rushey Drove, and upon the debt
being paid the registrar entered a satisfaction; the charge stands no longer.

Charge C-3303 was entered upon the title at Aldreth Green, and upon the tenant's
suit the court laid a stay upon its enforcement; the court afterward discharged
the stay, the suit having failed. A stay is a stopping of enforcement and not an
ending of the charge, and the charge stands in force.

Charge C-3304 was entered upon the title at Willow Holt, which the court vacated
upon proof that the deed was forged; the charge is set aside and stands no
longer.

Charge C-3305 was entered upon the title at Cotes Meadow, and stands, no motion
having touched it.

Charge C-3306 was proposed for entry upon the title at Turf Moor, but the
registrar requisitioned the application for want of the grantor's hand, and the
requisition stands unanswered; the charge is not enrolled and stands as no
charge.

## What stands

The charges in force, whose enrolments the register keeps, are C-3301, C-3303,
and C-3305. The charges C-3302 is satisfied and C-3304 vacated, their enrolments
put an end to, and C-3306 stands unentered upon its requisition; no enrolment or
copy of these remains in the registry.
