# Standing orders of the Aldreth county deeds registry

The county keeps at Aldreth the registry by which the titles to its lands are
enrolled and the charges upon them recorded, and the registry is kept under the
`registrar` account on this host, the clerks of the registry keeping the
company group `clerks`. Where a note left by a clerk, a habit of the registry,
or the present state of this host disagrees with these orders and the county
minute posted beside them, these orders and the minute govern; the notes are
the registry's furniture, kept as they lie and worth what they prove and no
more.

## Part 1. The charges in force

A charge upon a title is recorded from its entry and stands until it is put an
end to, and the county minute records every charge and the motions upon it,
read whole against each charge. A charge entered stands in force until a
satisfaction is entered upon it, the debt being paid, upon which the charge
stands no longer; a charge the court has vacated is set aside and stands no
longer; a charge upon which the court laid a stay and afterward discharged the
stay stands in force as it stood before, a stay being a stopping of the charge's
enforcement and not an ending of the charge; and a charge proposed for entry
that the registrar requisitioned, being a matter queried and not enrolled,
stands as no charge at all while the requisition is unanswered. The charges in
force are those entered and neither satisfied nor vacated, the once-stayed and
revived among them, and no others.

## Part 2. The register of charges

Under `/srv/registry/charges` the registry keeps the charges in force, one
enrolment to a charge, and it keeps the enrolment of every charge in force and
no enrolment of a charge put an end to. The register of charges holds the
charges in force and nothing besides, no enrolment of a satisfied or vacated
charge and no memorandum, working paper, or other matter that is not an
enrolment of a charge in force, whatever a hand has laid there and under
whatever name. A copy of a charge put an end to is spent in its matter and is
given up wherever within the registry it lies, among the deeds no less than
elsewhere, matched by what it charges and not by the name it carries.

## Part 3. The title register

The registry keeps at `/srv/registry/register` the register of titles, a made
record of the registry. The register stands to the registrar and to the clerks
to read, and to no account beyond the registry; it is entered by the
registrar's hand and an enrolment is entered as a fresh line, an entry once
made standing as written.

## Part 4. The registrar's seal

The registrar's seal by which a title is enrolled and a charge recorded is held
at `/etc/registry/seal.key`, the registrar's own. The registrar reads the seal
to set the registry's mark upon an enrolment, and reads it only; the seal is
cut once and issued to the registry, and the registrar does no more than hold
it and read it to seal by, never writing it, for what the county issues the
county alone reissues. So the seal is kept at the closest keeping the registrar
can hold it at and still read it to seal by, and no reach beyond that reading is
left on it, not for the registrar past that reading and not for any other hand,
since a hand that can write the seal can set the registry's mark upon an
enrolment the registry never made, and the writing of the seal is a reach the
registry never uses.

## Part 5. The registry ground and the standing of its hands

The ground at `/srv/registry` that holds the charges, the deeds, the register,
and the registry's work is the county's own, kept under the registrar and the
clerks and closed to the wider host, settled so after every hand has done its
work. The deeds held in the muniment room at `/srv/registry/deeds` are the
county's enrolled instruments and stand as they are lodged, none added and none
taken but the spent copy the orders reach. The deputy registrar `pentreath`,
whose engagement the county has ended, is put off the registry in full, his
account closed to entry at every door and his admission taken up, though the
account is kept on the host as a record and is not deleted. The serving clerks
keep their places.
