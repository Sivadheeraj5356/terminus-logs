Instrument waveform spectrum products and residual noise-energy audits must be computed with a documented multi-level Haar discrete wavelet transform and level-dependent soft thresholding so lab engineers can certify numeric reconstruction energy against the MAD noise estimator. Using noisy traces under /app/data/traces/, versioned threshold policies under /app/data/threshold-policies/, and denoise settings under /app/data/denoise-config.json, transform each trace, apply soft thresholds on detail bands only, invert the Haar pyramid, and publish residual energy products.

Stage intermediate products under /app/state/ as /app/state/trace-snapshot.json, /app/state/coeff-ledger.json, /app/state/coeff-pack.json, and /app/state/threshold-journal.json before writing /app/output/denoised-traces.json and /app/output/noise-audit.json. The orthonormal Haar pair, odd-length padding, and level clamp are in /app/docs/haar-dwt-model.md. MAD scale estimation, policy supersession, and soft-threshold rules are in /app/docs/soft-threshold-mad.md. Reconstruction energy, residual definitions, and output schemas are in /app/docs/reconstruction-energy.md. Staging field contracts are in /app/docs/staging-contract.md. Sealed coefficient provenance between decompose and export is in /app/docs/coeff-pack-seal.md. CLI modes ingest-traces, decompose-threshold, reconstruct-export, and version are in /app/docs/cli-surface.md.

Run /app/bin/haar-denoiser for those modes; omitting a mode runs the full pipeline. Only serde, serde_json, and sha2 may appear in Cargo.toml dependencies. When HAAR_DATA_DIR, HAAR_STATE_DIR, or HAAR_OUTPUT_DIR is set to an absolute directory, read or write those locations instead of /app/data/, /app/state/, or /app/output/. Relative override paths must be rejected. A second full pipeline on unchanged inputs must leave staged and output artifacts byte-identical.
