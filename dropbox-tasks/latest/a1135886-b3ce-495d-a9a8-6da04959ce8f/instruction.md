Build a Go program reading /app/chunk_inbox NDJSON shards and writing /app/output/expected.json. Envelope: chunk_id, tenant_id, object_id, chunk_offset, size_bytes, hash, content_fingerprint, algorithm, refcount, registered_ts, tier, seal. Optional: force_admit, force_reject, schema_version, op. Tables in /app/dedup_arena, /app/hash_codec, /app/tier_dialer, /app/quarantine_cell, /app/cycle_witness, /app/tenant_oracle, /app/object_graph.

The JSON has four top-level keys. chunks holds rows of chunk_id, object_id, registered_ts_ns, size_bytes, tenant_id, verdict, sorted by verdict severity descending then chunk_id ascending. Nineteen closed verdicts in severity order: VOIDED, REJECTED_CONTRADICTORY, INVALID, REJECTED_TYPE, REJECTED_SEAL, REJECTED_REPLAY, SCHEMA_DRIFT_QUARANTINED, QUARANTINED_TENANT, REJECTED_COLLISION, REJECTED_WEAK_HASH, REJECTED_DUPLICATE, DEFERRED_STALE, DEFERRED_COOLDOWN, DEFERRED_REHASH, DEFERRED_HOT, DEFERRED_BANDWIDTH, DEFERRED_RESERVED_OUT, DEHYDRATED, KEPT_CANONICAL. by_verdict zero-fills. by_tenant has one block per tenant in /app/tenant_oracle/tenant_map.psv with chunk_count (INVALID excluded), bytes_kept_canonical, bytes_dehydrated, top_verdict. summary holds total_chunks, run_ts_ns, bytes_kept_canonical, bytes_dehydrated, report_digest. /app/cycle_witness/output_contract.json pins types, digest formula, policy parameters.

Wrinkles: op void_next voids next same-tenant chunk (top); force_admit with force_reject: contradictory; HMAC-SHA256 seal via /app/hash_codec/hmac_secret.cfg; ts magnitude pivot; strict-int: unquoted decimal or quoted 0x/0o/0b, else reject; alias chain cycles invalid; prior-accepted chunk_id replays; unsupported schema_version drift; earliest-wins dedup on object+offset; half-open stale window plus per-tier grace; closed both-ends cooldown after reinstatement; past strong-hash review: rehash; weak-hash needs allowed tier plus fresh ts; hot threshold tier-scaled+floored, wrap-corrected via /app/cycle_witness/refcount_history.tsv; cross-tenant or fp-mismatch collides; budgets defer overages; bid ties to reserved-out; sticky overrides.
