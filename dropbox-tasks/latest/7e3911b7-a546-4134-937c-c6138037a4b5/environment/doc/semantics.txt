Volume tapes under /app/data/tapes describe grid dimensions, iso level, optional pad weighting, primitive fills (sphere, box), slab z boundaries, and an optional pause that snapshots and immediately resumes mid-run on the pause_resume matrix leg only. Slab extraction walks z layers and emits welded face keys. Pause boundaries must preserve weld hashes, slab z bases, and boundary face emission across resume. Pad weighting controls iso-tie preference for exact-iso cells; face packaging must stay stable across pad changes and resume so continuous and pause_resume digests agree with a cold continuous bake of the same tape.
