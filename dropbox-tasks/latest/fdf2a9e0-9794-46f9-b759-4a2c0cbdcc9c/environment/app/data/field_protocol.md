Cedar Fork flood routing field desk

The Cedar Fork channel network carries each flood wave reach by reach, and every reach stores and delays its flow through Muskingum channel-storage routing set by its storage time K and weighting factor X. The basin geometry, the reach K and X, the routing step dt_hours, the peak admission floor peak_min_cms, and the event headwater and lateral inflow marks (m³/s) all arrive packed in a little-endian binary field survey at /app/data/survey.bin. For one survey the desk returns, per event, the routed peak discharge (m³/s), the time-to-peak (h), and the outflow hydrograph (m³/s) at every admitted reach, advancing each reach outflow from its effective inflow across the marks. The Ruby field calculator /app/bin/channel-route reads a survey and prints these routed quantities as JSON on standard output. The survey byte layout, the Muskingum weight formulas, the rounding rule, and the quantity field and record order are in /app/docs/channel_hydrograph_contract.md. Each folder under /app/calibration/ holds one worked survey with its measured target quantities, and the same routing conventions apply to the held-out surveys evaluated during grading.
