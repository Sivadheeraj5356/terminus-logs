# Erratum E-2024-04 — Rescission of SMP-7 Revision C

Issued 2024-05-16. Prepared by the methods secretary on instruction of the
Dependency Review Board.

## Finding

Cross-validation of the March 2024 burst-event catalogue against the archive
station at Fenn Hollow showed systematic disagreement in reported band
powers. Three properties of revision C were implicated. First, the closed
band intervals combined with nearest-tenth rounding assign boundary bins to
two bands at once, so total reported power exceeds the frame's actual power
whenever energy sits at a band edge. Second, the plain segment-length
denominator adopted in revision C's §5 scales the density wrongly whenever
the window is not rectangular, overstating quiet-band power on tapered
segments.
Third, the removal of detrending lets slow fluxgate drift leak across the
quiet band.

## Resolution

Revision C of SMP-7 is rescinded in its entirety, effective on issue of this
erratum. Under SOP-9 §4 the station falls back to the most recent earlier
revision not itself rescinded. No revision of SMP-7 other than C has been
rescinded; the fallback is therefore revision B, and revision B remains in
force until the board adopts a successor.
