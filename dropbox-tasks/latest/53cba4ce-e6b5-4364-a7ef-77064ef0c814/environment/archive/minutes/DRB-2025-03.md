# Minute DRB-2025-03 — Dependency Review Board

Meeting of 2025-03-12. Present: T. Weil (chair), M. Anaya (secretary),
R. Ostrander, S. Okonkwo.

## Item 1 — Spectral methods, revision D

The board reviewed the methods secretary's draft prepared after the
2024–2025 burst-event reanalysis. The board resolved to adopt SMP-7
revision D, effective immediately, ending the interim force of revision B
that had obtained since erratum E-2024-04. The revision restores symmetric
windowing and window-energy scaling, retains half-open band intervals, and
extends the burst band's upper bound to 224 Hz.

## Item 2 — commons-math 4.x migration

R. Ostrander presented the evaluation of migrating the spectral service to
the commons-math 4.x line. The board acknowledged the technical case but
resolved that the migration question is deferred until after the Station
Systems Freeze; no 4.x artifact is approved by this minute, and the
`commons-math3` 3.6.1 approval of 2021 continues in force. The secretary
was asked to correct any contrary impression circulating in the engineering
journals.

## Item 3 — Freeze reminder

The chair reminded attendees that the Station Systems Freeze of 2025-11-30
set by SOP-9 fixes the decision record for audit purposes.

Minute confirmed by the chair, 2025-03-19.
