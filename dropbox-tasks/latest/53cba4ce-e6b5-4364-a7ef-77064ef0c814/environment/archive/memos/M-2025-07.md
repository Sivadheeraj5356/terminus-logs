# Memorandum M-2025-07 — Advisory register backlog

Date: 2025-11-03
From: M. Anaya, secretary
Disposition: adopted

## Purpose

Records, ahead of the Station Systems Freeze, the known gaps between
advisories issued and advisories registered in `registry.advisory_register`,
so that audit work performed against the freeze does not mistake the
register for the complete advisory record.

## Findings

The register presently carries four advisories. Two further advisories have
been issued in the station's history but were never entered in the register
and remain outside it as of this memorandum:

First, the mid-2022 advisory against the `chronolog` follow-up finding was
superseded within weeks by the vendor's corrected disclosure and was never
registered during its short life. Its recorded status is superseded. The
September 2022 engineering journal carries the only full account, including
the advisory's identifier and dates; this memorandum deliberately does not
restate them.

Second, advisory MRA-2025-05, issued 2025-10-20 concerning checksum
verification gaps in the station's mirror tooling, is still in intake
review. Its recorded status is pending, and under SOP-9 §5 a pending
advisory is not in force. Registration will follow intake; neither step is
expected before the freeze.

## Direction

The registration backlog is accepted as a known condition of the freeze
record. Dossier preparers must reconcile advisory counts across both the
register and the archive rather than relying on either alone.
