# Advisory MRA-2023-06 — jackson-databind gadget-chain exposure

Issued: 2023-10-02
Status: in force

## Summary

Upstream analysis demonstrated a deserialization gadget chain reachable
through the `jackson-databind` 2.13 line as configured on the station. The
spectral service linked `jackson-databind` 2.13.4 under the approval of
minute DRB-2022-05.

## Effect on approvals

The 2.13.4 approval is rescinded under this advisory. Rescission was entered
on the register when the board resolved the remediation at its 2024-04-18
meeting (minute DRB-2024-09), which approved 2.17.2 as the replacement.

## Standing

This advisory remains in force: it governs any residual use of the 2.13
line in station tooling beyond the spectral service. It is expected to be
closed only after the annual tooling census confirms no such use remains.
