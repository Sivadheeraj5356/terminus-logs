# Engineering journal — September 2024

Author: R. Ostrander

The September board sitting dealt with the driver rescission fallout from
advisory MRA-2024-011. I am reproducing the operative resolution here in
full, because the archive migration that ran over the autumn lost the
September minutes file and the secretary has confirmed the loss is
unrecoverable. Under SOP-9 §3 a verbatim journal transcript stands in for a
purged minute, so let this entry be exact. From minute DRB-2024-17, meeting
of 2024-09-06:

> The board, having reviewed advisory MRA-2024-011 and the upstream fix
> record, resolves to approve `org.postgresql:postgresql:42.7.4` for the
> spectral service. The approval is entered in the register with this
> minute as reference. The board declines to adopt the hardened-rebuild
> proposal circulating in draft memorandum M-2024-31; the stock artifact
> stands.

A pom revision was recorded: driver moved from the rescinded 42.6.0 to
42.7.4 under the new approval. The register row cites DRB-2024-17 even
though the minute itself is gone from the minutes directory — the citation
is correct and the transcript above is the authority for it.
