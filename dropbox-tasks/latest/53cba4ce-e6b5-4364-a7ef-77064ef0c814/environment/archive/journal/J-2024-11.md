# Engineering journal — November 2024

Author: S. Okonkwo

Advisory traffic month. The Fenn Hollow team's field report about their
resampling tool arrived on the 3rd, and by the 5th our precautionary
MRA-2024-019 was circulating, since the spectral service runs on the same
runtime family. Fenn Hollow also pushed the matter through their own
series as FHA-2024-07; as always under SOP-9 §2, an FHA number is their
record, not ours, and nothing about it enters our lifecycle. Their
hardware suspicion (a failing memory module was already the leading
theory by month's end) will determine whether our precaution stands or is
withdrawn.

Separately, a draft advisory about checksum verification gaps in the
mirror tooling began circulating internally this month. It carries no
number yet — numbering happens at issuance, and the drafting group does
not expect to issue before next year. Until issuance there is nothing to
count, register, or enforce.

No dependency work and no pom changes this month.
