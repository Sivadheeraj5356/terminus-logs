# Engineering journal — May 2025, first entry

Author: R. Ostrander

Emergency change on the 20th. The overnight ingest reconciliation threw
array-decoding mismatches on two channels and the on-call suspicion landed
on the 42.7.4 driver's binary array path. With the chair unreachable in the
field, I took the decision to drop the service back one driver release
while we investigated: 42.7.3 went into service that evening. Let the
record be clear that 42.7.3 has never held a board approval — this was an
emergency measure outside the approval process, taken under the operations
clause, and flagged for the board's next sitting.

A pom revision was recorded: driver moved from 42.7.4 to 42.7.3 as an
emergency measure pending investigation. The register carries a notation
row for 42.7.3 so the register reflects what actually ran, but a notation
is not an approval and the row says so.
