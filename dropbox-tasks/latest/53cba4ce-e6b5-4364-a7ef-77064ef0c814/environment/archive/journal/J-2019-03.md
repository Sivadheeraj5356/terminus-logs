# Engineering journal — March 2019

Author: R. Ostrander

Commissioning month for the spectral service. Stood up the meridian database
on the station host and loaded the first telemetry frames from the six
enrolled sites. The board sat on the 14th and approved the commissioning
dependency set — the 42.2.5 PostgreSQL driver and commons-math3 3.5 — and
adopted revision A of the methods procedure, all in minute DRB-2019-04.

Cut the first service build against those approvals.
A pom revision was recorded: the initial dependency set, driver 42.2.5 and
commons-math3 3.5, entered under the commissioning approval. Whole-frame periodograms look
noisy on the quiet channels, as the methods secretary warned they would;
filed a note to revisit segmentation once commissioning settles.
