Numeric archaeometallurgy assay of historical Au–Ag–Cu coins must recover physically valid bulk alloy mass fractions from non-destructive bulk-density and surface XRF measurements under a thermodynamic volume-additive mixing model, including full uncertainty-propagated feasible composition regions and inconsistency flags when density and corrected XRF bounds have empty intersection. Using the coin catalog at /app/data/coins.json, pure-metal reference densities at /app/data/metal-densities.json, surface-enrichment model table at /app/data/enrichment-models.json, and assay policy at /app/data/assay-config.json, evaluate each coin with the documented volume-additive mixing rule (or the supplied non-ideal correction term when a coin requests it), form the density-constraint band in composition space, apply the directional silver-bloom / copper-depletion surface-enrichment correction using each coin's own enrichment model parameters, intersect the density band with the corrected XRF bulk bound, and classify coins whose measurements admit no overlapping feasible region as measurement-inconsistent.

Persist the authoritative assay snapshot at /app/state/assay-snapshot.json. From that snapshot alone publish /app/output/composition-results.json (per-coin feasible region bounds or inconsistency / insufficient-data status), /app/output/derivation-trace.json (density band, corrected XRF bound, and intersection detail), /app/output/inconsistent-coins.json, and /app/output/audit.json. Normative mixing, density-band, surface-enrichment, intersection, missing-data, snapshot, results, and invocation contracts are in /app/docs/overview.md, /app/docs/mixing-rule.md, /app/docs/density-band.md, /app/docs/surface-enrichment.md, /app/docs/feasibility-intersection.md, /app/docs/missing-data-policy.md, /app/docs/snapshot-contract.md, /app/docs/results-schema.md, and /app/docs/invocation.md. Do not invent golden alloy tables; alternate coin catalogs, enrichment models, and absolute TB3_ASSAY_CONFIG paths may be supplied at runtime.

The binary at /app/bin/assay-ledger runs a full analyze-then-publish cycle by default. Modes analyze, check-coin with a coin id, export, and version are defined in /app/docs/invocation.md. Export without /app/state/assay-snapshot.json must exit 2 and must not re-read /app/data/; with a snapshot present it must regenerate the four output products from the snapshot alone after ledger and bind checks. Legacy helpers under /app/src/decoy/ are not on the assay hot path.
