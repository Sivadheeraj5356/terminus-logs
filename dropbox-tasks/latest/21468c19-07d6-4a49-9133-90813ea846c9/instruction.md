Build /app/analysis.R for the Mice Protein Expression multiclass task. Read /app/data/train.csv and /app/config/model_config.json, preserve the supplied fit, validation, and test roles, and learn every preprocessing choice from fit rows only. Use the closed-form one-vs-rest ridge score model described in /app/docs/07_model.txt, with softmax probabilities in the configured class order. Keep test labels out of model choice, preprocessing, lambda selection, and stability or refit decisions.

Write /app/outputs/metrics.json, /app/outputs/predictions.csv, /app/outputs/class_report.csv, /app/outputs/confusion_matrix.csv, /app/outputs/missing_report.csv, /app/outputs/validation_report.csv, /app/outputs/stability_refit_predictions.csv, /app/outputs/stability_refit_report.csv, /app/outputs/robust_lambda_consensus_report.csv, /app/outputs/feature_drop_refit_report.csv, /app/outputs/protein_pair_drop_refit_report.csv, /app/outputs/boundary_refit_report.csv, /app/outputs/paired_boundary_reselection_report.csv, and /app/outputs/model_manifest.json. Predictions are for test rows only and stay in row_id order. Treat blanks, literal question marks, and nan style markers as missing before numeric imputation or categorical level handling. Exact schemas, ordering, missing-value format, and manifest literals are in /app/docs/19_schema.txt.

The stability audit uses four deterministic refits of the original fit pool. For repeat_id 1 through 4, internal validation rows are original fit rows where row_id modulo 4 equals repeat_id minus 1; the remaining original fit rows train that repeat after relearning preprocessing. Combine the four stability winners with the supplied-validation winner as equal votes, then fit the consensus lambda on original fit rows for final outputs. The feature-drop refit report removes each numeric protein term one at a time, refits the consensus lambda on fit rows, and ranks the validation log-loss and macro-F1 shifts. protein_pair_drop_refit_report.csv repeats that refit audit for the documented adjacent protein pairs. n_terms is the number of retained design-matrix terms after the drop, including the intercept and every remaining numeric or categorical indicator term. The boundary refit report audits the lowest-margin test cases, removes the nearest conflicting fit example, refits the consensus lambda, and reports probability and decision shifts. The paired boundary reselection report uses the same audited test rows, removes both the nearest conflicting fit row and the nearest fit row that supports the baseline predicted class, relearns preprocessing, reselects lambda on the unchanged validation split, and reports the local probability and decision shift.
