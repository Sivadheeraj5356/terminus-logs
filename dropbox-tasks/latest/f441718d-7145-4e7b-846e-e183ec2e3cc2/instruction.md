A machine-learning model-training orchestration plane under /app runs an offline supervised training workflow for tabular feature ingest, schema normalization, feature-store sync, gradient-boosted model fitting (train), holdout evaluation (eval), artifact publish, and compliance audit. The sample AI training workflow under /app/testdata/workflows/ materializes a model-training DAG with feature, train, evaluate, and publish nodes so experiment lineage, training-checkpoint recovery, deterministic training replay, and a signed ML recovery certificate stay auditable across eight ordered machine-learning stages A→H.

Stage contracts for training-workflow parsing, feature-DAG compilation, deterministic train/eval scheduling, training-checkpoint selection/merge, failed-run recovery, epoch-relative training replay, lineage verification, and HMAC recovery certification are defined under /app/docs/ (including /app/docs/ml-overview.md, /app/docs/pipeline.md, and /app/docs/stage-a.md through /app/docs/stage-h.md). Policy and the signing token live under /app/configs/. Required artifacts after a successful training run include /app/state/normalized.json, /app/state/compiled.json, /app/state/schedule.json, /app/state/execution.json, /app/state/journal.json, /app/state/checkpoints.json, /app/state/recovery.json, /app/state/recovered_checkpoint.json, /app/state/replay.json, /app/state/verification.json, /app/output/certificate.json, /app/output/certificate_status.json, /app/output/verification.json, /app/output/recovery.json, and /app/output/replay.json.

The learning coordinator under /app/cmd/runtime and /app/internal/ must satisfy those machine-learning contracts so the A→H training workflow materializes the listed artifacts with byte-identical experiment digests on repeated clean runs. Soft flags must not replace recomputed training digests or HMAC verification of the recovery certificate. The Go toolchain is available at /usr/local/go/bin/go; after source changes the binary at /tmp/runtime must reflect the updated sources because evaluation rebuilds from /app. Binary-only substitution without matching Go sources under /app is insufficient.
