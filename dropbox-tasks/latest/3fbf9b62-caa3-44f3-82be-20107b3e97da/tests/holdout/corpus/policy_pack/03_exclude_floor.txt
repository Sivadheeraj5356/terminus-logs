Exclude overlay and toolchain floors (signed)
---------------------------------------------
Pins whose module_path appears in the exclude overlay are excluded. Excluded pins keep
measured retract, checksum, and floor fields, but gate_action is pass, policy_basis is
exclude_skip, effective_violation is false, and block_reason is empty.

Each pin declares go_floor. The manifest declares toolchain_declared. Floor satisfaction
is decided by the gofloor helper named in the manifest. CLI: gofloor <toolchainDeclared>
<pinFloor>. Stdout match with exit 0 means go_floor_ok. Comparison is dotted numeric
major.minor.patch; a leading go prefix on either side is ignored.
