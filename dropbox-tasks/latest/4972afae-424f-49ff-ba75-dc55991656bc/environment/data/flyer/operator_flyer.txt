                          S K I T T L E   A L L E Y

                       THE CLASSIC GAME OF KAYLES
                       NOW ON YOUR SITE

A row of skittles stands on the alley. The player bowls, and the ball knocks
down one pin or two. If the ball goes in among the standing pins the row is
broken in two and the game runs on both halves at once. The house bowls back.
The side that knocks down the last pin standing wins the alley.

It is the oldest bowling puzzle there is and every player thinks they know it.
Very few of them beat the house.

  CABINET      upright, 25 inch monitor, two player start
  BOARDSET     SKAL-0001
  GAME         one or two rows of skittles, one ball, no timer
  PLAY         knock down one pin or two, split the row or leave it standing
  WIN          take the last pin
  EARNINGS     tested at 380 plays a week on site
