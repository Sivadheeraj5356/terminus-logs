BROADWATER AMUSEMENT CO - SKITTLE ALLEY BOARD REVISIONS

SKAL-0001-A
  The production board. Roughly four thousand were built between the autumn of
  1981 and the spring of 1982. The game program is the one described in the
  operator flyer.

SKAL-0001-B
  Built from the spring of 1982 until the line closed. The board carries a
  larger scratch memory, a ball dial and a throw latch that the A board does
  not have, and a revised game program that reads them. Cabinet art, marquee,
  attract sequence, high score card and operator flyer were not changed, and
  the flyer shipped with the B cabinets is the flyer written for the A board.
  Collectors report that the two boards do not always play alike once a game
  moves off the opening rack, and the factory never issued a note.

The board in this cabinet is a SKAL-0001-B.
