SKITTLE ALLEY SERVICE LINK

The cabinet exposes a line-oriented service link on its standard input and
output. Each command is one line. Each reply is one or more lines followed by
a line reading END.

COMMANDS

  RACK <dial> <latch> <pins> [<pins>]
      Sets up a fresh rack. The dial face and the latch code are set to the
      values given. One or two lanes may be racked. The practice cabinet only
      sets up racks inside the service band it can tabulate; a rack outside the
      band is refused.

  SHOW
      Reports the live rack.

  THROW <lane> <pins> <split>
      Sends a throw down the given lane. The lane is numbered from 1 in the
      order the lanes are reported. The pin count is the number of pins the
      ball takes. The split field is 0 for a throw that leaves the standing
      remainder as one lane, or the pin count of the left fragment when the
      remainder is to be broken in two.

  HOUSE
      The house takes its turn immediately.

  QUIT
      Drops the link.

REPLIES

  READY <dial> <latch> <lanes> [<pins> ...]
      A rack was set up.

  RACK <dial> <latch> <lanes> [<pins> ...]
      The live rack.

  OK <dial> <latch> <lanes> [<pins> ...]
      The throw was accepted. The dial and the latch are reported as they stand
      after the throw and before the house replies.

  REJECT
      The throw was not accepted. The rack is unchanged.

  HOUSE <lane> <pins> <split>
      The house threw.

  RESULT PLAYER
  RESULT HOUSE
      The rack is clear. The side that cleared it took the last throw and the
      side with no throw left loses.

  REFUSED BAND
      The rack is outside the practice cabinet's service band.

  REFUSED ARGS
  REFUSED IDLE
      The command was malformed, or no rack is live.
