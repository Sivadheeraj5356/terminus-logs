ATTRACT SEQUENCE INDEX

The ROM holds ten demonstration replays. The cabinet plays them in order while
it waits for a coin. Every one of them is a single lane racked from the ball
dial's home face with the throw latch clear, which is the state the cabinet
resets to when it powers on, and every one of them was recorded on this board.

  demo_01.log   5 pins
  demo_02.log   6 pins
  demo_03.log   8 pins
  demo_04.log   9 pins
  demo_05.log  11 pins
  demo_06.log  13 pins
  demo_07.log  14 pins
  demo_08.log  16 pins
  demo_09.log  18 pins
  demo_10.log  19 pins

A replay line reading THROW names the side that threw, the lane, the pins the
ball took and the split. A replay line reading STATE reports the dial, the
latch and the pins left standing.
