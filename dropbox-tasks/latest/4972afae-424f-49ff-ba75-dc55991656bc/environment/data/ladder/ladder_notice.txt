BROADWATER TOURNAMENT LADDER - NOTICE TO ENTRANTS

The ladder is bowled on the tournament rig, not on the practice cabinet. The
rig racks alleys of a size no site cabinet ever sets up, it sets the ball dial
to any face and the throw latch to any code before the first ball is thrown,
and it racks one lane or two. The rig's house bowls the alley out and it does
not miss a winning line.

An entrant is dealt each alley once. It calls the alley before it bowls: an
alley that cannot be taken from the entrant's side of the table is conceded,
and an alley that can be taken is called and then bowled out against the house.
Calling an alley that cannot be taken is a lost match, conceding an alley that
could have been taken is a lost match, and a throw the rig will not accept ends
the match on the spot.

The entrant is the program at /app/entry. The rig speaks to it over the link
written up in entry_link.txt.
