Play the CipherGrid season-4 puzzle-league match on the terminal board. Players score moves from the season archive under `/app/data/season4/` (cells, glyphs, move tape, published scorecard). Run `/app/bin/cipherleague score --archive DIR --out REPORT.json` to tally the match and write the sealed scorecard. Combo streaks, rivalry clashes, and dotted echo edges on the board map follow `/app/data/season4/scorecard.md` and the match rules under `semantics` in `/app/schema/ciphergrid-puzzle-league.schema.json`.

Per-move base points equal glyph weight plus twice the count of orthogonally adjacent same-family solution cells, rounded half-away-from-zero to two decimals. Multiply that by pressure `1.0 + 0.05 * R` where `R` counts same-family solution cells absent from earlier tape rows, then round again before summing; round each player total the same way. Combo streaks group a player's moves by tick then cell_id and grow when consecutive solution families match; each continued step after the first adds `0.25 * (streak_len - 1)` before rounding `combo_points`. Rivalry awards `0.5` to each side whenever two different players' moves sit within one tick, on orthogonal neighbors, with a shared solution family; emit rounded `rivalry_points` and `rivalry_digest`. Dashed board-map edges mark same-family adjacencies; dotted echo edges mark same-family adjacencies where both cells appear on the tape.

The scorecard lists `players` (`player_id`, `points`, `moves`, `combo_points`, `rivalry_points`) sorted by `player_id`, plus `season_score_digest`, `combo_digest`, `rivalry_digest`, `board_fingerprint`, `layout_digest` from digraph `ciphergrid` (solid / dashed / dotted arcs; digest hashes Graphviz plain-layout `name|x|y` at two decimals), `playtest_seal` as SHA-256 of `season_score_digest|layout_digest|combo_digest|board_fingerprint`, `match_chain_digest` as SHA-256 of `prior|playtest_seal|rivalry_digest` with prior from `/app/var/match_chain_tip.txt` (empty when absent) then tip rewrite, and `match_crown` as SHA-256 of `playtest_seal|rivalry_digest|match_chain_digest`.
