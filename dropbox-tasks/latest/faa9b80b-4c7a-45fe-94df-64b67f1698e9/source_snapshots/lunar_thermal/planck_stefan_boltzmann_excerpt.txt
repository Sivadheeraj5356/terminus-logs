Planck spectral radiance (wavelength form):
B_lambda(lambda,T) = 2*h*c^2 / (lambda^5 * (exp(h*c/(lambda*k_B*T)) - 1))
Stefan–Boltzmann emitted flux: eps * sigma * T^4
Constants used in solver_config.json (CODATA-aligned):
- h = 6.62607015e-34 J s
- k_B = 1.380649e-23 J/K
- c = 299792458 m/s
- sigma = 5.670374419e-8 W/m^2/K^4
Task uses trapezoidal band integration and brightness-temperature root finding without dividing observed radiance by emissivity during T_b inversion.
