# Composition contract

For every pair of eligible observations, kinship points are 3200 for equal nonempty malware, 2600 for equal nonempty beacon, 2200 for equal canonical contractor, and 1800 for equal toolcell with overlapping half-open intervals. Pair support is floor((sum of kinship points) times min effective reliability divided by 10000). Candidate support requires support at least `edge_threshold_bp` and two distinct source names across the pair.

A commodity beacon equality vetoes that pair even when other support exists. Different nonempty actors create an exclusivity contradiction when intervals overlap. Vetoed or contradictory candidate pairs become contradiction cuts with sorted `left`, `right`, `reason`, and `support_bp`; they never connect campaigns. The `reason` string for a commodity beacon veto is exactly `commodity-hosting-veto`. The `reason` string for an actor exclusivity contradiction is exactly `actor-exclusivity`. Non-cut candidate edges form connected components.

Composition minimizes false association cost before maximizing cohesion. For each connected component, repeatedly consider non-cut edges ordered by ascending false cost, descending support, then endpoint IDs. False cost is `10000 - support_bp`. An edge joins two sets only if it adds no commodity or actor contradiction between any members. The selected forest defines campaigns. This lexicographic ordering is authoritative.

Campaign IDs are `campaign-` plus the first 12 hexadecimal characters of SHA-256 over comma-joined sorted member IDs. Cohesion is the sum of selected edge support. Confidence basis points are floor(total effective reliability of members divided by member count), plus min(1500, 250 times the number of distinct sources beyond one), capped at 10000. Bands are high at or above `high_confidence_bp`, medium at or above `medium_confidence_bp`, otherwise low. Support witnesses list selected edges and their sorted distinct source names. Singleton eligible observations and ineligible observations appear in the unassigned frontier with reason `no-admissible-support` or `below-reliability`.
