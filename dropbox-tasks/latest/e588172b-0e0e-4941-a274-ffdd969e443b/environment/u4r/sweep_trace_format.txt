Fragment wire format consumed by the resume window.

Each fragment line carries four pipe-separated fields:

    day_key | wall_off | seq | flags

wall_off at or above 86400 marks a boot-gap fragment. On the initial rebuild,
with boot_era.rec still absent, that fragment is emitted by the first pass with
seq below 100. Recovery (seq at or above 100) is a later-step path that only
runs once boot_era.rec already exists.

The flags field is a bitmask. Bit 0x04 marks a fragment that was skipped
because its interval never occurred on the local wall clock during a spring
forward transition. A fragment carrying that bit keeps its single first-pass
row and must not be emitted again by recovery.
