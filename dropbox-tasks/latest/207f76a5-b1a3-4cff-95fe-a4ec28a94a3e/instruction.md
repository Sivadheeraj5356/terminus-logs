Spatial autocorrelation asks whether nearby areal units resemble one another more than chance allows. Here a map of U.S. counties carries two attributes (a binary label, black (1) or white (0), and an integer numeric value) together with a disclosed binary contiguity edge list that defines the first-order neighbour matrix W1. We want three classic diagnostics computed exactly and read off the same weight geometry, so that the neighbour structure is shared and any error in it propagates.

For the binary label we want the Cliff-Ord weight sums S0, S1 and S2, the observed black-black, white-white and black-white boundary counts under the undirected half-edge convention, and, under both the free-sampling null (each unit independently black with probability n1/n) and the nonfree conditional null (the count of black units held fixed), the exact expectation, variance and a two-sided verdict for each join count. Under that conditional null we also want the exact two-sided permutation p-value of each join count, obtained by enumerating the equally likely labellings whenever their number is small enough to enumerate. For the numeric value we want Moran's I and Geary's C with their exact randomization expectation and variance (the Cliff-Ord moments that carry the sample kurtosis b2) and a two-sided verdict.

Every statistic is reported as the weight geometry changes: on W1, on the second-order lag-2 contiguity W2 (units sharing a first-order neighbour but not adjacent, a boolean two-step closure with the diagonal and W1 removed, never a tally of two-paths), and, for Moran's I, on the row-standardized W1, whose asymmetric rational weights give S0 equal to the non-isolated count rather than 2M. A constant attribute, an empty W2, or a monochromatic map is reported with the sentinel NA.

All arithmetic is exact rational with no floating point; the gmp package is available. Results come from Rscript /app/join_count.R DIR, where DIR holds units.csv and edges.csv (an example map is at /app/data). The exact schema, the weight constructions, the moment formulas, the reported lines and their order, and the NA and ERROR rules are set out in /app/docs/SCHEMA.md and /app/docs/OUTPUT-CONTRACT.md.
