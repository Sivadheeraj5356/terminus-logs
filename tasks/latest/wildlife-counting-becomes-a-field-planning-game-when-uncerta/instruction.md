Wildlife counting becomes a field-planning game when uncertain trails, fatigue, changing forecasts, animal disturbance, and locked reports all compete for the same short expedition. Guide two explorer pairs across separate habitats, collect enough repeat sightings to trust each count, and bring every report home safely. The opening play separates one tracker and locks the first weak estimate.

Each expedition arrives as an active census URL for /app/safari_dispatch.mjs. That file holds the player's choices; all other files under /app remain fixed.

Win every holdout expedition in open_savanna, river_confluence, and thornveld_dusk with status complete, end_reason census_submitted, and at least 17800 points. Points reflect count accuracy, route assessment, paired movement through risk, fatigue, disturbance, observation conditions, elapsed turns, and the four-decision turn limit. Continue the supplied census rather than opening another.

The field handbook at /app/safari_runtime.md covers visible habitats, forecasts, permitted moves, and scoring.
