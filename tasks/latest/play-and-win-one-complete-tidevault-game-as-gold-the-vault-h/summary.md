## Summary

Play and win one complete Tidevault game as Gold. The vault has three rings, I, M, and O, crossed by six sectors, A through F.

Gold and Violet each own three one-use pieces at each of powers one, two, and three. An action such as P2 IA M CW places a power-two piece into the empty slot IA, then rotates ring M one step; clockwise sends A to B, while counterclockwise sends A to F.
