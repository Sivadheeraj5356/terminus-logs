# Advisory MRA-2021-01 — chronolog appender key handling

Issued: 2021-02-03
Status: closed (2021-03-30)

## Summary

The station's build of the `chronolog` logging appender, used briefly by the
telemetry ingest tooling, wrote its rotation key material to a
world-readable path. The exposure was confined to the ingest host; the
spectral service never linked the affected artifact.

## Disposition

Ingest tooling was rebuilt without the appender and the key material was
rotated. The advisory was closed on 2021-03-30 after verification. Closed
advisories are retained in the register for the permanent record and are
not in force.
