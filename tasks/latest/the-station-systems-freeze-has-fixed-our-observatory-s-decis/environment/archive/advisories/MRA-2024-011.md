# Advisory MRA-2024-011 — PostgreSQL JDBC parameter interpolation exposure

Issued: 2024-08-19
Status: in force

## Summary

A confirmed upstream disclosure showed that PostgreSQL JDBC driver releases
earlier than 42.7.2 can, under specific parameter-encoding conditions, allow
crafted input to alter statement structure. The station's exposure is
aggravated by service code that assembles SQL by string concatenation, a
practice separately prohibited by memorandum M-2023-19.

## Effect on approvals

Every board approval of an `org.postgresql:postgresql` version earlier than
42.7.2 is rescinded under this advisory. At issue, two approved versions on
the register fell under that threshold — the 42.2.5 approval of 2019 and the
42.6.0 approval of 2023 — and both were marked rescinded. No version at or
above 42.7.2 held an approval on the issue date of this advisory.

## Standing

This advisory remains in force. It governs the approved-version floor for
the driver: no rescinded version may be linked by station services, and any
new driver approval must be at or above 42.7.2.
