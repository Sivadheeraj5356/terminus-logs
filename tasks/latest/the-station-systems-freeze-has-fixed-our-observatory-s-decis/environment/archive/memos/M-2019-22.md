# Memorandum M-2019-22 — Station channel identifiers

Date: 2019-06-17
From: M. Anaya, secretary
Disposition: adopted

## Purpose

Fixes the naming convention for telemetry channels in the meridian database.

## Convention

Channel identifiers derive from the survey name of the instrument site,
lowercased, with spaces replaced by underscores and all other punctuation
retained verbatim. The convention deliberately preserves punctuation so that
identifiers remain reconcilable against the original survey ledger.

Six sites were enrolled at adoption: Ridge East, Ridge North, Saddle West,
O'Neill Ridge NW, Bench South (VLF), and Terrace Central. Under the
convention the O'Neill Ridge NW fluxgate is carried as `o'neill_nw` — the
apostrophe is part of the identifier. Engineering was reminded that any code
touching channel identifiers must treat them as opaque text, not as
material for statement assembly.
