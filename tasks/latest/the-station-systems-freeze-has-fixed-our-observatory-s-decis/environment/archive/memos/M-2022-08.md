# Memorandum M-2022-08 — JSON serializer candidates for the spectral service

Date: 2022-03-28
From: S. Okonkwo
Disposition: adopted (carried into minute DRB-2022-05)

## Purpose

Recommends a JSON serialization library for the spectral service's reporting
endpoints, for board decision.

## Evaluation

Three candidates were exercised against the service's response shapes:
`jackson-databind`, `gson`, and hand-rolled serialization. Hand-rolled
serialization was rejected on maintenance grounds after the prototype
mishandled non-finite values. `gson` handled the shapes correctly but its
release cadence had slowed. `jackson-databind` handled all shapes, carries
the widest tooling support, and the 2.13 line was under active maintenance
at evaluation time.

## Recommendation

Approve `com.fasterxml.jackson.core:jackson-databind:2.13.4`. The board
adopted this recommendation at its meeting of 2022-04-11.
