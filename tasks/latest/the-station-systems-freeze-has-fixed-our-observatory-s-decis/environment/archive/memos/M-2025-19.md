# Memorandum M-2025-19 — Outline for a future SMP-7 revision E

Date: 2025-11-21
From: F. Delacroix-Hume
Disposition: informal outline — no standing

## Purpose

Circulates, for discussion after the freeze, an outline of changes the
methods group may propose as SMP-7 revision E.

## Sketch

The outline would extend the burst band's upper bound from 224 Hz to
240 Hz, following this autumn's high-band observations, and would double
the analysis segment length from 1024 to 2048 samples to sharpen low-band
resolution, with the segment advance kept at half the segment length.
Window choice and scaling would carry over from revision D unchanged.

## Standing

This is an outline, not a revision. It has not been drafted as procedure
text, has not been brought to the board, and confers no force on any
parameter stated above. Revision D, adopted 2025-03-12, is the revision in
force, and the freeze fixes the audit record against revision D. Anyone
preparing a dossier against the freeze should disregard this outline
entirely; it is archived only so the winter discussion has a written
starting point.
