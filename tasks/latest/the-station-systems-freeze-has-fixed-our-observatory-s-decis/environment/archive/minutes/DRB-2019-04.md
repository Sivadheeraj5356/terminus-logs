# Minute DRB-2019-04 — Dependency Review Board

Meeting of 2019-03-14. Present: T. Weil (chair), M. Anaya (secretary),
R. Ostrander, F. Delacroix-Hume.

## Item 1 — Commissioning dependency set for the spectral service

The board resolved to approve `org.postgresql:postgresql:42.2.5` as the
database driver for the spectral service. The board resolved to approve
`org.apache.commons:commons-math3:3.5` as the numerical library. Both
approvals were entered in the artifact review register with this minute as
reference.

## Item 2 — Spectral methods

The board resolved to adopt SMP-7 revision A as the station's spectral
methods procedure, effective immediately.

## Item 3 — Register discipline

The secretary noted that approvals take effect on board resolution and that
the register is to carry one row per reviewed version, updated in place as
statuses change. The board concurred.

Minute confirmed by the chair, 2019-03-21.
