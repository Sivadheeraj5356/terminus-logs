# Minute DRB-2023-11 — Dependency Review Board

Meeting of 2023-05-09. Present: T. Weil (chair), M. Anaya (secretary),
R. Ostrander, S. Okonkwo, F. Delacroix-Hume.

## Item 1 — Database driver refresh

Returning to the item held open since 2021, the board reviewed the driver
series and resolved to approve `org.postgresql:postgresql:42.6.0` for the
spectral service. The 42.2.5 approval of 2019 remains on the register; the
board expressly declined to rescind it, so that instruments still carrying
the older driver stay within policy while migrations complete.

## Item 2 — Spectral methods

The board resolved to adopt SMP-7 revision C, accepting the methods
secretary's argument that the station's processing budget justified the
reduced overlap and simplified scaling. R. Ostrander recorded a dissent
regarding the closed band intervals; the dissent is noted without effect on
the resolution.

## Item 3 — Query construction practices

S. Okonkwo raised concern that portions of the service assemble SQL by
string concatenation. The board requested a memorandum; no resolution was
taken at this meeting.

Minute confirmed by the chair, 2023-05-16.
