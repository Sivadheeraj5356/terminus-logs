# Minute DRB-2021-08 — Dependency Review Board

Meeting of 2021-07-22. Present: T. Weil (chair), M. Anaya (secretary),
R. Ostrander, S. Okonkwo.

## Item 1 — Numerical library refresh

The board reviewed the maintenance record of `commons-math3` release 3.6.1
against the 3.5 release approved at commissioning. The board resolved to
approve `org.apache.commons:commons-math3:3.6.1` for the spectral service;
the 3.5 approval is marked superseded in the register. The board noted, for
the avoidance of doubt, that supersession is not rescission: 3.5 was retired
in good standing.

## Item 2 — Spectral methods

The board resolved to adopt SMP-7 revision B, which introduces overlapped
segmentation, window-energy scaling, and per-segment detrending. Revision A
is superseded.

## Item 3 — Database driver

R. Ostrander asked whether the 42.2.5 driver approval should be refreshed.
The board took no resolution; the item was held for a future meeting.

Minute confirmed by the chair, 2021-07-29.
