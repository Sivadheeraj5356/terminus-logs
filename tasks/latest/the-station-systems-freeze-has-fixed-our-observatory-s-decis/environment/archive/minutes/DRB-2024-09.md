# Minute DRB-2024-09 — Dependency Review Board

Meeting of 2024-04-18. Present: T. Weil (chair), M. Anaya (secretary),
S. Okonkwo, F. Delacroix-Hume.

## Item 1 — JSON serializer remediation

Advisory MRA-2023-06 (issued 2023-10-02) stands in force against the
`jackson-databind` 2.13 line. The board resolved to approve
`com.fasterxml.jackson.core:jackson-databind:2.17.2` for the spectral
service. The 2.13.4 approval is marked rescinded on the register under the
advisory. The advisory itself remains in force as it governs any residual
use of the affected line elsewhere on the station.

## Item 2 — Driver series watch

The secretary reported upstream disclosure activity around parameter
handling in the PostgreSQL JDBC driver series and recommended the board
prepare for a rescission decision. The board directed the secretary to
draft an advisory should the disclosure be confirmed.

Minute confirmed by the chair, 2024-04-25.
