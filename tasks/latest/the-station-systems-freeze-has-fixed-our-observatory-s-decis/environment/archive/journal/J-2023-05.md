# Engineering journal — May 2023

Author: R. Ostrander

Board month (minute DRB-2023-11). The driver refresh finally moved: 42.6.0
approved for the service, with the old 42.2.5 approval deliberately left
standing so the tide-gauge tooling stays within policy while it migrates.
Two driver approvals on the register at once — first time that's happened.

A pom revision was recorded: driver bumped from 42.2.5 to 42.6.0 under the
new approval. The board also adopted methods revision C over my recorded
dissent; I reworked the estimator to the reduced overlap and the plain
length scaling as adopted, and flagged in the code that the closed band
intervals will double-count any energy sitting exactly on a band edge.
Okonkwo raised the string-concatenation problem in the data access layer
and the board asked for a memo rather than resolving anything.
