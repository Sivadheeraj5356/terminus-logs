# Engineering journal — May 2025, second entry

Author: R. Ostrander

Closing out the emergency of the 20th. The array-decoding mismatches
reproduced with the driver pinned back and eventually traced to a schema
drift in the ingest staging table — nothing to do with the driver at all.
The 42.7.3 emergency measure bought us nothing and was unwound on the 28th.

A pom revision was recorded: the emergency change reverted, driver restored
from 42.7.3 to the approved 42.7.4. That makes this the first recorded pom
revision whose sole purpose was to revert an earlier recorded one, and I
hope it stays the only such pair. The board was briefed by note; no minute
was raised, since the end state matches the standing approval.
