# Engineering journal — February 2025

Author: F. Delacroix-Hume

Spent the month on the commons-math 4.x evaluation with Ostrander. The
4.x line splits the old monolith into modules and the FFT surface we use
maps over cleanly; my benchmark harness showed parity on our segment
sizes. Presented the findings informally at the pre-board review and the
room was warm to it — as I read it, the board endorsed moving the spectral
service to math4, and I have started sketching the module layout for the
migration branch.

No dependency changes went in this month; the migration would be a matter
for a future pom revision once the paperwork catches up. Revision D of the
methods procedure is also circulating in draft ahead of the March sitting —
symmetric windows back, burst band out to 224.
