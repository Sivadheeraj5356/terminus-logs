# Engineering journal — September 2022

Author: R. Ostrander

Quiet month for the service itself; the record-keeping was the work. Two
advisory matters closed out. First, the chronolog follow-up finding that had
been circulating since June as advisory MRA-2022-03 (issued 2022-06-14) was
superseded on 2022-09-01, when the vendor retracted the June disclosure and
replaced it with a corrected one that does not reach our configuration. The
advisory had a life of eleven weeks and was never entered in the register —
intake was queued behind the summer maintenance and by the time the queue
drained there was nothing left in force to register. Its recorded status is
superseded. Second, Fenn Hollow circulated FHA-2022-11 on the same vendor
disclosure through their own advisory series; under SOP-9 §2 a Fenn Hollow
advisory is not a station advisory and takes no place in our lifecycle, so
it is noted here for context only.

One dependency note for completeness: a driver from the 42.5 line was
exercised on the bench in June while we chased a connection-pool question.
The bench trial ended in July; the version was never brought to the board,
never entered service, and the register carries no row for it. It should
not be confused with the approvals record.

No pom changes this month; the recorded revision history stands where the
April entry left it.
