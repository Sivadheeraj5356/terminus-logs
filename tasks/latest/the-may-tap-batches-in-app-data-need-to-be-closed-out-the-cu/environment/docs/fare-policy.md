# Fare policy notes

The tap files under `/app/data/taps/` are newline-delimited JSON. Each record has `tap_id`, `rider_id`, `service_date`, `tapped_at`, `route_id`, `station_id`, `direction`, and `device_status`. Late tap correction files under `/app/data/corrections/` are JSONL records with `correction_id`, `tap_id`, `field`, `value`, and `approved`. CSV feeds use normal quoted CSV fields, so quoted values may contain commas. Preserve identifier text exactly after parsing. Temporary service-zone adjustment files under `/app/data/zone-adjustments/` are CSV files with `zone_adjustment_id`, `service_date`, `route_id`, `station_id`, `direction`, `service_zone`, and `approved`. Pass ledgers under `/app/data/passes/` are CSV files with `rider_id`, `pass_id`, `service_zone`, `active_from`, `active_to`, and `status`. Subsidy ledgers under `/app/data/subsidies/` are CSV files with `rider_id`, `subsidy_id`, `service_zone`, `active_from`, `active_to`, `percent`, `monthly_limit_cents`, and `status`. Subsidy recovery files under `/app/data/subsidy-recoveries/` are JSONL records with `recovery_id`, `rider_id`, `subsidy_id`, `service_month`, `amount_cents`, and `approved`. Exception credit files under `/app/data/exceptions/` are JSONL records with `exception_id`, `tap_id`, `scope`, `amount_cents`, and `approved`, where `scope` is `base`, `surcharge`, or `both`. Settlement hold files under `/app/data/holds/` are JSONL records with `hold_id`, `rider_id`, `service_date`, `route_family`, `scope`, `amount_cents`, and `approved`, where `scope` is `base`, `surcharge`, or `both`. Carry-forward credit files under `/app/data/carryovers/` are JSONL records with `carryover_id`, `rider_id`, `earned_service_date`, `expires_service_date`, `route_family`, `scope`, `amount_cents`, and `approved`, where `scope` is `base`, `surcharge`, or `both`; they may also include an optional `daily_limit_cents` value. A carryover `route_family` value of `all` means the row can reduce every matching route family for the rider-day. Credit reversal files under `/app/data/credit-reversals/` are JSONL records with `reversal_id`, `target_kind`, `amount_cents`, and `approved`; carryover reversals also include `rider_id` and `carryover_id`, while account-credit reversals include `account_id` and `credit_id`. Route-family minimum CSV files under `/app/data/minimums/` have `minimum_id`, `service_date`, `rider_id`, `route_family`, `min_base_cents`, `min_express_cents`, and `status`. Cap exclusion files under `/app/data/cap-exclusions/` are JSONL records with `exclusion_id`, `rider_id`, `service_date`, `route_family`, `amount_cents`, and `approved`. Account link CSV files under `/app/data/accounts/` have `rider_id`, `account_id`, `active_from`, `active_to`, and `status`. Account credit files under `/app/data/account-credits/` are JSONL records with `credit_id`, `account_id`, `earned_service_date`, `expires_service_date`, `scope`, `amount_cents`, and `approved`, where `scope` is `base`, `surcharge`, or `both`; they may also include optional `rider_id`, `route_family`, and `service_zone` filters, plus an optional `daily_limit_cents` value. Manual adjustment files under `/app/data/adjustments/` are JSONL records with `adjustment_id`, `rider_id`, `service_date`, `amount_cents`, `reason`, and `approved`. Service disruption files under `/app/data/disruptions/` are JSONL records with `disruption_id`, `service_date`, `route_family`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `credit_cents`, and `approved`. Fare override files under `/app/data/overrides/` are CSV files with `override_id`, `tap_id`, `action`, `amount_cents`, and `approved`.

The SQLite database at `/app/contracts/fare_policy.sqlite` is the policy source of truth. It has `riders(rider_id, rider_type)`, `rider_type_rules(rider_type, discount_percent)`, `routes(route_id, service_zone, route_family, base_cents, express_surcharge_cents, active)`, `stations(station_id, station_zone, active)`, `daily_caps(rider_type, service_zone, cap_cents)`, `account_daily_caps(account_id, service_zone, cap_cents)`, `transfer_rules(from_family, to_family, minutes)`, and `station_pair_surcharges(surcharge_id, from_station_id, to_station_id, route_family, amount_cents, active)`.

Approved late tap corrections are applied before sorting taps, checking unresolved status, or calculating fares. Valid correction fields are `rider_id`, `route_id`, `station_id`, `device_status`, `tapped_at`, and `service_date`. If more than one approved correction has the same `tap_id` and `field`, use the row with the lexically greatest `correction_id`. Apply `rider_id`, `route_id`, `station_id`, `device_status`, and `tapped_at` corrections before service-date normalization. A `service_date` correction is a billing service-date override: after deriving the normalized service date from corrected `tapped_at`, replace that normalized date with the corrected `service_date` value. Ignore unapproved corrections, unsupported fields, and corrections for tap ids that are not in the tap files. Count a tap in `corrected_tap_count` only when at least one supported approved correction changes one of that tap's final corrected values.

After approved late tap corrections are selected and applied, normalize every tap's billing service date from the corrected `tapped_at` value. The raw `service_date` field is provisional. Local taps with a corrected local time from `00:00:00` through `02:59:59` belong to the previous calendar service date; all other taps belong to the calendar date in `tapped_at`. If the selected late correction for that tap includes `field` equal to `service_date`, use that corrected service date instead of the normalized date. Use the final billing service date everywhere below: tap sorting, rider-day grouping, transfer grouping, unresolved events, pass and subsidy date checks, disruption date checks, manual adjustment matching, daily caps, account caps, every output CSV, and the summary JSON. Service-day normalization by itself does not count as a late tap correction for `corrected_tap_count`.

A fareable tap must have `device_status` equal to `accepted`, an existing rider, an active route, and an active station after late tap corrections are applied. Other taps belong in the unresolved list with reason `rejected_device`, `unknown_rider`, `unknown_route`, or `inactive_station`. Each unresolved event in the summary must contain exactly `tap_id`, `rider_id`, `service_date`, and `reason`.

Approved temporary service-zone adjustments apply only to fareable taps, after late corrections and unresolved classification. A row matches by final billing `service_date`, corrected `route_id`, corrected `station_id`, and `direction`; a direction value of `all` is a fallback that matches any direction for that route and station. Ignore unapproved rows, rows for unresolved taps, rows for other service dates, and rows whose route or station does not match after corrections. Prefer exact-direction matches over `all` matches. If more than one approved row remains at the same specificity, use the row with the lexically greatest `zone_adjustment_id`. The selected `service_zone` replaces the route table's `service_zone` only for zone-dependent policy checks: pass coverage, rider subsidy matching, daily cap selection, account cap selection, and account credit `service_zone` filters. It also contributes to the rider-day and route-family zone sets used by those cap and credit rules. It does not change route family, base fare, express surcharge, transfer grouping, station-pair surcharge matching, disruption matching, tap sorting, corrected route_id, or any output schema.

Approved fare overrides apply only to fareable taps. A `base_override` replaces the route `base_cents` for that tap before the rider discount is calculated. A `surcharge_override` replaces the route `express_surcharge_cents` for that tap. A `station_pair_surcharge_override` replaces only the matched station-pair surcharge amount for that tap. A `suppress_pass` means active pass coverage must be ignored for that tap. If more than one approved override has the same `tap_id` and `action`, use the row with the lexically greatest `override_id`. Ignore unapproved overrides and overrides that point at unresolved taps.

Station-pair surcharges apply only to fareable taps after route surcharge overrides are selected and before exception credits. Look at the previous fareable tap for the same rider and normalized service date, using the corrected station ids and the corrected processing order. If an active `station_pair_surcharges` row matches that previous tap's station as `from_station_id`, the current tap's station as `to_station_id`, and the current route's `route_family`, add `amount_cents` to the current tap's express surcharge. If multiple active rows match, use the row with the lexically greatest `surcharge_id`. If the current tap has an approved `station_pair_surcharge_override`, use that override amount instead of the matched station-pair amount. If there is no previous fareable tap for that rider-day, no active station-pair row matches, or the matched station-pair row is inactive, add no station-pair surcharge. Station-pair surcharges do not affect base fare, rider discounts, transfers, passes, disruption credits, subsidies, or daily caps.

For a fareable tap, apply any approved base override, then apply the rider discount and round to the nearest cent using half-up integer rounding: `(base_cents * discount_percent + 50) // 100`. Transfers are evaluated per rider and service date in tap time order. If the previous fareable tap is within the matching transfer window for its route family into the current route family, the current discounted base fare is waived before checking pass coverage. An active pass covers the discounted base fare only when the fare was not already waived, the pass status is `active`, the service date is inside the pass date range, the pass zone is either the route zone or `all`, and the tap does not have an approved `suppress_pass` override. Passes do not cover express surcharges.

Approved service disruption credits apply after transfer waivers and pass coverage, before rider subsidies and daily capping. A disruption matches a fareable tap when `service_date` and `route_family` match and the local `tapped_at` time is greater than or equal to `start_time` and less than `end_time`. Add the `credit_cents` for all matching approved disruptions, reduce only the remaining base charge by that amount, and clamp the reduction so the base charge never goes below zero. Track the actual reduction as `disruption_credit_cents`. Disruption credits do not reduce express surcharges and do not apply to unresolved taps or base fare that was already waived or covered.

Active rider subsidies apply after disruption credits and before daily capping. A subsidy is active only when `status` is `active`, the tap service date is between `active_from` and `active_to`, and `service_zone` is either the route zone or `all`. If more than one active subsidy matches the same tap, use the row with the lexically greatest `subsidy_id`. Calculate the requested subsidy as `(remaining_base_cents * percent + 50) // 100`, reduce only the remaining base charge, and track the actual reduction as `subsidy_credit_cents`. The `monthly_limit_cents` is a cap per rider, subsidy, and calendar month, consumed in fareable tap order by `service_date`, `rider_id`, `tapped_at`, and `tap_id`. Before processing taps for a month, approved subsidy recoveries for that same `rider_id`, `subsidy_id`, and `service_month` consume the matching monthly limit by `amount_cents`. Sum all approved recovery rows for the same key. Ignore unapproved recoveries and recoveries for other months or unmatched subsidies. Recoveries never add charges directly, never count as manual adjustments, and do not appear as their own output field; they only reduce the limit available to later tap subsidy credits in that month. Once a subsidy reaches its monthly limit, later taps get no more credit from that subsidy. Subsidies do not reduce express surcharges, manual adjustments, unresolved taps, or base fare that is already zero.

Approved exception credits apply after rider subsidies and station-pair surcharges, and before daily capping. They apply only to fareable taps. Ignore unapproved exception rows and rows pointing at unresolved taps. For each tap, sort matching approved exception rows by `exception_id` and apply them in that order. A `base` credit reduces only the remaining base charge, a `surcharge` credit reduces only the remaining express surcharge including any station-pair amount, and a `both` credit reduces the remaining base charge first, then any remaining express surcharge. Clamp each reduction so neither charge goes below zero, and track the actual combined reduction as `exception_credit_cents`.

Approved settlement holds apply after exception credits and before daily capping. They are route-family-level credits, not per-tap credits. A hold matches only the same `rider_id`, final billing `service_date`, and route family. Ignore unapproved holds and holds for a route family that has no fareable taps for that rider-day. Apply approved holds for the same rider, date, and route family in `hold_id` order. A `base` hold reduces that route family's remaining base charge, a `surcharge` hold reduces that route family's remaining express surcharge, and a `both` hold reduces base first and then any remaining express surcharge. Clamp each hold to the remaining amount available in that route family. Track the actual reduction as `hold_credit_cents` on the rider-day output and summary. Holds do not change `base_before_cap_cents`, `express_before_cap_cents`, or any other tap-audit charge-stage field because they are applied after per-tap audit values are finalized.

Approved carry-forward credits apply after settlement holds and before route-family minimums and daily capping. They are route-family-level credits, not per-tap credits. A carryover matches only the same `rider_id`. It can be used only on service dates later than `earned_service_date` and no later than `expires_service_date`. Ignore unapproved carryovers, expired carryovers, same-day carryovers, and carryovers that match no route family with fareable taps on that rider-day. Process rider-days in final billing service-date order. Within a rider, apply eligible carryovers by `earned_service_date` and then `carryover_id`; any unused balance stays available for later eligible service dates. A row with a concrete `route_family` only reduces that route family. A row with `route_family` equal to `all` can reduce all route families for that rider-day. Optional `daily_limit_cents` limits how much that carryover can spend on one service date; missing, empty, and `all` mean no per-date limit. A `base` carryover reduces remaining base charge, a `surcharge` carryover reduces remaining express surcharge, and a `both` carryover reduces base first, then any remaining express surcharge. For an `all` carryover, allocate each stage reduction across matching route families in proportion to their current remaining amount in that stage. Start with floor division, then distribute leftover cents to the largest fractional remainders, breaking ties by `route_family`. Clamp each reduction to the matching remaining charges, the carryover's remaining balance, and the carryover's remaining daily allowance. Track the actual reduction as `carryover_credit_cents` on the rider-day output and summary. Carryovers do not change `base_before_cap_cents`, `express_before_cap_cents`, or any other tap-audit charge-stage field because they are applied after per-tap audit values are finalized.

Approved credit reversals are applied once before any service date is processed. A row with `target_kind` equal to `carryover` matches a carry-forward credit by `rider_id` and `carryover_id`. A row with `target_kind` equal to `account_credit` matches an account credit by `account_id` and `credit_id`. Sum approved reversal amounts for the same target and subtract that amount from the target's starting balance, clamping the starting balance at zero. Ignore unapproved reversal rows, unsupported target kinds, and rows that do not match any approved credit. Reversals never add charges, never count as manual adjustments, do not spend daily limits, and do not appear in any output field except through reduced carryover or account-credit totals.

Active route-family minimum rows apply after settlement holds and carry-forward credits, before daily capping. A row matches only the same final billing `service_date`, `rider_id`, and route family, and only when `status` is `active`. If more than one active row matches the same route family, use the row with the lexically greatest `minimum_id`. Ignore rows for route families that have no fareable taps on that rider-day. Raise that route family's remaining base charge to at least `min_base_cents` and remaining express surcharge to at least `min_express_cents`, adding only the difference needed to reach each minimum. Minimum top-ups are route-family-level charges, not credits. They do not change `base_before_cap_cents`, `express_before_cap_cents`, any tap-audit charge-stage field, any credit total, unresolved counts, or adjustment totals. Base minimum top-ups are included before individual and account caps are calculated. Express minimum top-ups are not capped.

Approved cap exclusions apply after route-family minimums and before individual daily caps. A cap exclusion matches only the same final billing `service_date`, `rider_id`, and route family. Ignore unapproved exclusions and exclusions for route families that have no fareable taps on that rider-day. Apply approved exclusions for the same rider, date, and route family in `exclusion_id` order. Each row marks up to `amount_cents` of that route family's remaining base charge as cap-excluded base, clamped to base that has not already been excluded. Cap-excluded base remains charged in rider-day totals and route-family `base_charged_cents`, does not change any credit total, and does not change any tap-audit charge-stage field. It is not counted when checking or allocating individual daily caps, and it is not counted in account-cap base totals or account-cap allocation. Later account credits may still reduce cap-excluded base because those credits apply after all caps.

After transfer waivers, pass credits, disruption credits, subsidy credits, exception credits, settlement holds, carry-forward credits, route-family minimum charges, and cap exclusions, cap each rider-day's remaining cap-eligible base charges using the highest applicable cap for the zones used by that rider on that service date. Express surcharges and cap-excluded base are not capped.

For route-family reporting, keep each fareable tap's route family, remaining base charge after all per-tap credits, and remaining express surcharge including any station-pair surcharge before daily capping. Apply settlement holds, carry-forward credits, route-family minimum charges, and cap exclusions to those route-family base and express totals before checking the rider-day cap. If a rider-day is capped, calculate the rider-day cap credit as `cap_eligible_base_before_cap - capped_cap_eligible_base_total` after hold, carryover, minimum, and cap-exclusion handling. Allocate that cap credit across route-family rows in proportion to each route family's cap-eligible base charge. Start each allocation with integer floor division, then distribute any leftover cents one at a time to the rows with the largest fractional remainder, breaking ties by `route_family` in lexicographic order. Route families with zero cap-eligible base charge receive no cap credit. Manual adjustments and unresolved taps are not assigned to route-family rows.

After individual rider-day caps have been allocated, apply account-level daily caps. A rider is linked to an account only when an account link row has `status` equal to `active` and the final billing service date is between `active_from` and `active_to`, inclusive. Ignore pending and inactive links. For each account and service date, gather linked rider-days that have fareable taps. Determine the service zones used by those linked fareable taps after all corrections and billing-date normalization. Choose the highest `account_daily_caps.cap_cents` row for that account whose service zone was used by any linked fareable tap that day. If no matching cap row exists, do not apply an account cap. Compare the cap only to linked route-family cap-eligible base charges after individual daily cap allocation, excluding cap-excluded base. If the linked cap-eligible base total exceeds the account cap, allocate `linked_cap_eligible_base_total - account_cap` across the linked route-family rows with positive post-individual-cap cap-eligible base charge. Use integer floor division, then distribute leftover cents to the largest fractional remainders, breaking ties by `rider_id` then `route_family`. Account caps reduce only cap-eligible base charges and are applied before approved manual adjustments are added. They do not reduce cap-excluded base, express surcharges, unresolved taps, manual adjustments, or any tap-audit charge-stage field. In `account-cap-ledger.csv`, `base_before_account_cap_cents` is the linked cap-eligible base total before account cap allocation.

Approved account credits apply after account-level daily caps and before approved manual adjustments. A credit matches an account only by `account_id`, can be used only on service dates later than `earned_service_date` and no later than `expires_service_date`, and applies only to rider-days with fareable taps and an active account link for that service date. Ignore unapproved credits and credits for accounts that have no linked fareable rider-days on a service date. Optional `rider_id`, `route_family`, and `service_zone` filters restrict which linked route-family rows a credit may reduce. Missing filters, empty strings, and `all` mean no restriction. A filtered credit must match every present filter. The `service_zone` filter matches a route-family row if at least one fareable tap in that row used that route service zone. An optional `daily_limit_cents` field limits how much that credit can reduce on a single service date; missing, empty, and `all` mean no per-date limit. The daily limit is shared by base and surcharge reductions for that credit on that date, so a `both` credit spends it on base first and only the leftover daily allowance can reduce express surcharge. Process service dates in final billing order. Within an account, use credits by `earned_service_date` and then `credit_id`; any unused approved amount stays available for later eligible service dates, but unused daily allowance does not add to another date's daily allowance. A `base` credit reduces linked remaining base charges, a `surcharge` credit reduces linked remaining express charges, and a `both` credit reduces base first, then any remaining express. For each account-date and stage being reduced, allocate the credit across the matching linked rider route-family rows in proportion to the amount still remaining in that stage. Use integer floor division, then distribute leftover cents to the largest fractional remainders, breaking ties by `rider_id` then `route_family`. If a credit has no matching remaining charge on a service date, leave its balance for a later eligible service date. Clamp each reduction to the remaining amount for that stage, to the credit's remaining overall balance, and to the remaining daily allowance for that service date. Track the actual reduction as `account_credit_cents` on rider-day and route-family outputs and as `total_account_credit_cents` in the summary. Account credits do not change tap-audit charge-stage fields, account-cap ledger rows, unresolved counts, or manual adjustments.

Write `/app/output/rider-day-settlement.csv` with header `service_date,rider_id,total_charged_cents,tap_count,distinct_routes,capping_applied,pass_credit_cents,disruption_credit_cents,subsidy_credit_cents,exception_credit_cents,hold_credit_cents,carryover_credit_cents,account_cap_credit_cents,account_credit_cents,adjustment_cents,unresolved_count`. Include any rider-day with a fareable tap, an approved adjustment, or an unresolved tap. The `tap_count` and `distinct_routes` values count only fareable taps after unresolved taps have been separated. The `distinct_routes` value counts unique corrected `route_id` values, not route-family values. Unresolved taps only affect `unresolved_count` and the summary unresolved fields. Use integer cents, `true` or `false` for `capping_applied`, and sort by service date then rider id.

Write `/app/output/route-family-ledger.csv` with header `service_date,rider_id,route_family,base_charged_cents,express_charged_cents,cap_credit_cents,account_cap_credit_cents,account_credit_cents,tap_count`. Include one row for each service date, rider, and route family with at least one fareable tap. `base_charged_cents` is the route family's remaining base charge after settlement holds, carry-forward credits, route-family minimum charges, allocated individual daily cap credit, allocated account cap credit, and account credits. `express_charged_cents` is the sum of remaining express surcharges for that route family after station-pair surcharges, exception credits, settlement holds, carry-forward credits, route-family minimum charges, and account credits. `cap_credit_cents` is the allocated individual daily cap credit for that route family. `account_cap_credit_cents` is the allocated account-level cap credit for that route family. `account_credit_cents` is the allocated account-credit reduction for that route family across base and express charges. `tap_count` counts fareable taps in that route family. Sort by service date, rider id, then route family.

Write `/app/output/tap-audit-ledger.csv` with header `tap_id,service_date,original_tapped_at,tapped_at,original_rider_id,rider_id,original_route_id,route_id,original_station_id,station_id,original_device_status,device_status,unresolved_reason,route_family,base_after_discount_cents,transfer_credit_cents,pass_credit_cents,disruption_credit_cents,subsidy_credit_cents,exception_credit_cents,base_before_cap_cents,express_before_cap_cents`. Include one row for every raw tap after late tap corrections have been selected. The audit `service_date` is the normalized billing service date, not necessarily the raw `service_date` from the tap feed. Sort rows by service date, corrected rider id, corrected tapped_at, then tap id. The `original_*` fields must preserve the raw tap values before corrections, while the unprefixed tap fields must show values after supported approved corrections. For unresolved taps, set `unresolved_reason` to the policy reason, set `route_family` to an empty string, and set every charge-stage field to `0`. For fareable taps, leave `unresolved_reason` empty, set `route_family` from the route table, set `base_after_discount_cents` to the discounted base fare before transfer, pass, disruption, subsidy, exception, or cap effects, set `transfer_credit_cents` to the discounted base fare waived by a transfer, set the per-tap pass, disruption, subsidy, and exception credit fields to the actual reductions applied at that stage, set `base_before_cap_cents` to the remaining base charge after all per-tap credits but before daily cap allocation, and set `express_before_cap_cents` to the remaining express surcharge after route surcharge overrides, station-pair surcharges, and exception credits.

Write `/app/output/account-cap-ledger.csv` with header `service_date,account_id,service_zone,base_before_account_cap_cents,account_cap_cents,account_cap_credit_cents,participating_rider_days`. Include one row for each account-date where the account cap reduces base charges. `service_zone` is the zone of the selected `account_daily_caps` row. `base_before_account_cap_cents` is the linked base total after individual rider-day caps and before account cap allocation. `participating_rider_days` counts linked rider-days with fareable taps that contributed to the account-date group. Sort by service date then account id.

All output CSV files must be valid comma-delimited CSV. Quote any field that contains a comma, quote, carriage return, or newline, and escape an embedded quote by writing two quote characters.

Write `/app/output/settlement-summary.json` as pretty-printed JSON with `service_dates`, `rider_day_count`, `total_charged_cents`, `total_pass_credit_cents`, `total_disruption_credit_cents`, `total_subsidy_credit_cents`, `total_exception_credit_cents`, `total_hold_credit_cents`, `total_carryover_credit_cents`, `total_account_cap_credit_cents`, `total_account_credit_cents`, `total_adjustment_cents`, `capped_rider_days`, `account_capped_groups`, `total_fareable_taps`, `corrected_tap_count`, `unresolved_by_reason`, and `unresolved_events`. Sort unresolved events by service date then tap id.
