A computational fluid dynamics lab stages two finite-volume Burgers simulation consumable kits across a season of shock-capture and flux-calibration campaigns. Weekly simulation demand for each kit is known in advance; purchase prices move week to week. Stock carries forward in a shared cold store, each week's runs are met from stock on hand, and end-of-week inventory for every kit must stay at or above that kit's safety reserve `smin[p]` in every week.

Kits differ in refrigerated-truck volume; instance capacity `B` limits volume per run. The lab may keep trucks on the books for the season or call outside hauliers when weekly demand exceeds owned capacity. Fleet, holding, peak, and shock-capture fees are documented under `/app/docs`, `/app/consumables`, and `/app/facilities`. Procurement indexes and finance worksheets in those trees may disagree—reconcile before modelling. Inventory balance and reserve rules are in `/app/consumables/handling.md`.

Build a minimum-cost MiniZinc constraint model at `/app/model.mzn` reading `/app/instance.dzn`. Print weekly purchases as `q1=[...]` and `q2=[...]` per `/app/docs/output_format.md`. The starter declares only `T`, `P`, `B`, `v`, `d`, `c`, and `smin`; read `/app/docs/data_dictionary.md` for the full instance schema, required parameter names in the model, and modeling notes. Hidden and battery instances not shipped in the image use the same schema. Samples in `/app/samples` match `/app/instance.dzn`. Run `/app/run_model.sh`.
