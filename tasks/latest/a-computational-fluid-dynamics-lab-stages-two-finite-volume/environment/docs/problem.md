# Problem: minimum-cost Burgers FV consumable staging

Multi-week finite-volume Burgers equation simulation campaigns require staging two consumable kit lines with per-kit safety reserves, shared refrigerated-truck capacity, and cold-store holding. Campaign-window and peak-load fees are documented across `/app/docs`, `/app/consumables`, and `/app/facilities`.

Build a minimum-cost MiniZinc model. See `/app/docs/data_dictionary.md` for the instance schema and modeling notes.
