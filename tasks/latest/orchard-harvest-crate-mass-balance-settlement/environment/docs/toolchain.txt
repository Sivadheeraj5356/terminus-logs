HARVEST1 orchard metrology numerical mass-balance settlement toolchain

The pick-balance settlement engine evaluates HARVEST1 orchard capture feeds under the gram-conservation metrology model in harvest_rules.txt and emits per-zone mass accounting and grade-band reports with deterministic checkpoint-resume equivalence to cold replay.

Driver build: make -C /app
Driver binary: /app/bin/pick-balance
Driver flags: --input, --out, --checkpoint-in, --checkpoint-out
Container roots: ORCHARD_APP_ROOT and TB_APP_ROOT both default to /app.
Rust toolchain: channel 1.85.0 (see rust-toolchain.toml).
