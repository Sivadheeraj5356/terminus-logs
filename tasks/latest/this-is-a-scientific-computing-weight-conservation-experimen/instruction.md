This is a **scientific computing** weight-conservation experiment for conserved-mass projection estimators. Under `/app`, a scientific measurement harness (`cryoready`) runs scripted Monte Carlo-style conservation trials over a cryogenic bay vessel pool, projects conserved usable-capacity fill mass, hold-time mass after reserve, absolute-MAWP pressure gauges, and specimen-placement eligibility mass onto digest-bound inventory checkpoints, then resumes assess materialization so estimator-bias diagnostics can be compared against an uninterrupted monolithic bay baseline. **Scientific computing** success means conserved fill/hold/pressure mass, maintenance-window and calibration gauges, cryogen-class compatibility estimators, preferred-placement projection estimators, certificate integrity gauges, and durable ledger continuity remain valid under checkpoint-resume — when any of those disagree with the monolithic baseline, the measurement is scientifically invalid. This is a scientific measurement-validation experiment, not a software-engineering or debugging exercise.

In plain terms: every usable liter of cryogen mass that should count toward fill fraction and hold-time must still be accounted for after reserve subtraction, six-decimal rounding, absolute PSIA conversion, half-open maintenance windows, and exact-epoch sensor expiry; every specimen placement request must conserve eligibility mass onto ready compatible vessels; certificates and ledger counters must not invent or lose assess mass across truncated-file salvage. A fill projection estimator is `level_liters / usable_capacity_l` (not gross) rounded to six decimals; a hold projection estimator subtracts `usable_capacity_l * hold_reserve_fraction` before boil-off hours; checkpointing writes `/app/state/inventory-stage.json` bound to input digests so a later assess leg continues without inventing or losing vessel mass. On the shipped bay-alpha experiment fixtures those estimators are scientifically invalid for usable vs gross fill, reserve hold-time, absolute MAWP conversion, maintenance end-boundary, exact-epoch sensor expiry, LHe-into-LN2 compatibility, preferred-ineligible assignment, certificate gating, and composite ledger keys.

Make `/app/bin/cryoready` emit scientifically valid pool-ledger summaries that honor the **scientific computing** conservation laws in `/app/docs/CONTRACT.md`. Trial-feed and gauge semantics are in `/app/docs/facility-schema.md`, `/app/docs/catalog-schema.md`, `/app/docs/maintenance-schema.md`, and `/app/docs/inventory-stage.md`; placement projection, certificate, and ledger estimators are in `/app/docs/readiness-report.md`, `/app/docs/certificate-contract.md`, and `/app/docs/ledger-contract.md`; measured CLI surfaces are in `/app/docs/cli-surface.md` and `/app/README.md`. Experiment fixtures live under `/app/fixtures/`.

Drive conservation trials with `cryoready inventory --facility <abs> --catalog <abs> --maintenance <abs>` writing the digest-bound inventory checkpoint under `/app/state/`, and resume materialization with `cryoready assess --facility <abs> --catalog <abs> --maintenance <abs> --output-report <abs> --output-certificate <abs>`. Inventory must write a stage whose vessel metrics and ready reasons match the conservation laws. Assess must refuse missing or stale stages with exit status 2 mentioning inventory, emit report and certificate fields/digests that match the docs, satisfy placements only with compatible ready vessels that meet hold-time, set `certificate_ok` only when every placement is satisfied, and persist ledger counters under composite `facility|catalog|maintenance` digest keys while advancing `write_seq` on every successful save and recovering salvaged entries from a truncated ledger file.
