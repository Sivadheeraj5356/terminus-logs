# operator argv for pp_run

Working tree: /app/environment
Driver: /app/h7p/pp_run
Same driver file: /app/environment/h7p/pp_run
Desk root variable desk or ENV_ROOT defaults to /app/environment for staged rebuilds.

Rebuild every rotation from rot_tbl.toml and write /app/output/policy_pack/:

  /app/h7p/pp_run rebuild

Rebuild a single rotation id:

  /app/h7p/pp_run rebuild --id r04

Smoke health probe (ledger totals only):

  /app/h7p/pp_run health

Make wrappers (same phases):

  make -C /app/environment rebuild
  make -C /app/environment health

Rebuild walks each rotation in rot_tbl.toml, stages per-rotation work under the
desk tree, then writes pack members under /app/output/policy_pack/. Closed-row
algebra, preamble peer rules, pin-seed coupling, and seal obligations are in
ctr_rules.md. Smoke shortcuts that keep ledger totals green are in
operator_notes.md.
