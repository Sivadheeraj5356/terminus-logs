Diviner Lunar Radiometer Experiment (DLRE) measures lunar surface thermal emission in multiple infrared channels.
Relevant interpretation for this task:
- Multi-band thermal radiometry informs brightness temperatures used as inversion observables.
- Synthetic bands in this task (band_8um, band_25um) are compact stand-ins for mid-IR Diviner-like channels.
- Units: radiance W/m^2/sr; brightness temperature K.
Intentionally excluded: full mission calibration pipelines, roughness beaming, and real observation databases.
