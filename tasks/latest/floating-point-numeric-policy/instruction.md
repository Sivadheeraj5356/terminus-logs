A seeded machine-learning training harness under `/app/environment` (Node) runs optimizer update loops with first/second moment accumulators and a learning-rate schedule over local fixture data, and supports halt-and-continue training. Resumed training must reproduce the same learning curves as an uninterrupted training run over the same inputs. After a halt-and-continue cycle, `/app/output/stage` headline totals and `/app/environment/fixtures/interim/q3_hold.json` can look acceptable while the graded training audit still shows the resumed curve disagreeing with the uninterrupted curve for the same inputs.

Update the training implementation under `/app/environment` so that a normal regeneration of the training audit outputs satisfies the public contracts. Driver-only tweaks, hand-editing `/app/output`, or static/manual JSON writes are not enough; the verifier rebuilds and reruns the training pipeline from source.

The graded witness JSON includes a summary section that reports which fixtures and profiles were exercised and how many fixture-profile pairs ran, plus a rows array of per-mode audit entries.

Commands, row schema, numeric limits, and recovery steps are in `/app/environment/guide/pol_table.md` and `/app/environment/guide/out_shape.md`. Primary fixture ids are in `/app/environment/guide/id_list.txt` (`ash`, `elm`, `oak`, `fir`). Authoritative bytes are under `/app/environment/fixtures/frames/`; log tails under `/app/environment/fixtures/logs/` are partial.

After rebuild, the full audit must exit 0 for all primary fixtures under both profiles plus held-out pairs from `/app/environment/data/hold_q3.toml`. Graded output is `/app/output/resume_witness.json` using the row schema in `/app/environment/guide/out_shape.md` (including `mode`, `curve_digest`, `band_drift`, and `band_tail`). The verifier reruns rebuild, driver phases, and the full audit before checking that file. Training audit agreement depends on the load-bearing modules listed in `/app/environment/guide/pol_table.md`; bypassing those modules is not enough.
