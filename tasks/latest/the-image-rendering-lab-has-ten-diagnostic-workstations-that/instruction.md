The image-rendering lab has ten diagnostic workstations that can no longer host Valgrind or gdb sessions because a recent image refresh left them with an unsafe mix of ptrace, core-dump, group-membership, ulimit, and filesystem-permission settings. Your job is to author an Open Policy Agent Rego library under `/app/policy/` that translates each workstation's compliance state, sourced from the DuckDB inventory at `/app/inventory/workstations.duckdb` (view `v_workstation`), into an ordered remediation plan that matches the canonical decision matrix documented in `/app/postmortem/postmortem.md`. The postmortem is long but complete; every rule, every exact action string, and the required ordering are stated there.

Your library must declare `package workstation.plan` and expose `actions` at `data.workstation.plan.actions`. For every workstation the emitted `{"workstation_id": <id>, "actions": [...]}` object must carry the id and the ordered action strings exactly as the postmortem prescribes, drawn from the seven documented action families (sysctl, systemd, group:add, ulimit, perms, fsattr, fixture-perms) with no duplicates, with all sysctl actions preceding any non-sysctl action, and with the workload-appropriate fixture-perms action as the terminal entry. All ten workstations must have their emitted plans exactly match their corresponding golden JSON files under `/app/golden/`.
