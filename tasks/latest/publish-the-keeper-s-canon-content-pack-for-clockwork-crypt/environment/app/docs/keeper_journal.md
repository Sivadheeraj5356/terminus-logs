# Keeper Journal: Clockwork Crypt design, rehearsals, and ratification

This is the chronological working record used by the Clockwork Crypt team. Room nicknames are common, and proposals often survive in the book after they are rejected. An adopted motion remains operative until a later signed motion explicitly reopens the same subject. Rehearsal observations, staging conveniences, comparison seals, and unsigned margins do not amend canon. The keeper's closing construction states which late folios form the final migration contract, including inherited identities and the endgame performance ledger.

The journal deliberately retains discarded maps, object chains, dialogue cues, performance tuples, and art proofs because they explain the fixture history. Read decisions in context: nearby notes may describe what was tested rather than what was adopted, and exact database symbols are not interchangeable with similar literary phrases.
