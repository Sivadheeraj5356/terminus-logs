# Note left by the deputy registrar

For the next hand at the registry. I left the enrolments as they were, the
satisfied and vacated among them, since the parties sometimes come back and it
saved the re-enrolling. A working memorandum of mine I kept in the charges
register where I could reach it. A fair copy of the Rushey Drove charge I laid
among the deeds against the mortgagee's asking, filed under a plain leaf. The
seal I kept in the drawer, readable and writable under the account, which saved
re-cutting the mark when it dulled. The title register I left open for the
clerks to enter their own day's enrolments. Leave it as it stands and the
registry keeps itself.
