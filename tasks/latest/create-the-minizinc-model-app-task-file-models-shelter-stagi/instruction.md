Create the MiniZinc model `/app/task_file/models/shelter_staging.mzn`. The verifier runs it with Gecode against public and additional compatible instances materialized from the schema below.

Each instance supplies every field in `/app/task_file/instance_schema.json`: pallet count `P`, intake waves `W`, shelter zones `Z`, volunteer teams `T`, handling classes `H`, per-pallet `pallet_count`, `protection`, `mandatory`, `handling_class`, `arrival_minute`, `process_minutes`, and `deadline_minute`, zone/team eligibility matrices, wave/zone/team capacities, dependency parent indices, conflict-pair arrays, the scheduled-count band, minimum occupied wave/team cells, directed `class_changeover` minutes, per-class `class_min_waves`, and per-team `team_wave_limit`. Parent index zero means no dependency. The public instance is complete and follows the same contract as generated instances; do not add fallback values in the model.

Declare output decision arrays named `wave`, `zone`, and `team`, indexed `1..P`, plus integer `objective`. Zero in all three arrays means a pallet is unscheduled. A scheduled pallet uses wave `1..W`, zone `1..Z`, and team `1..T`; its zone and team must be eligible. Mandatory pallets are scheduled. Scheduled counts stay within `min_scheduled..max_scheduled`; the sum of `pallet_count` assigned to each wave, zone, and team stays within the corresponding capacity. If pallet `p` has a parent, scheduling `p` also schedules its parent no later than `p`. Both members of a conflict pair may be scheduled but may not share a wave. At least `min_cells` distinct wave/team pairs must be occupied.

Every occupied wave handles exactly one `handling_class`, so all pallets in that wave have the same class. Occupied waves form a nonempty prefix starting at wave 1. Each class `h` must occupy at least `class_min_waves[h]` waves. A team may appear in at most `team_wave_limit[t]` distinct waves. The setup before wave 1 is `class_changeover[h,h]` for its class; each later occupied wave adds the directed changeover from the preceding wave's class to its class. Call their sum `setup_total`.

The single intake bay processes occupied waves sequentially. A wave's duration is the maximum `process_minutes` among its pallets. Its start is no earlier than every pallet's `arrival_minute` and, for wave 1, its initial setup; a later wave starts no earlier than the previous finish plus its directed class changeover. Every scheduled pallet's wave must finish by that pallet's `deadline_minute`. `makespan` is the maximum wave finish. Because the objective penalizes makespan, an optimal solution uses the earliest feasible starts for its chosen assignment.

Set `objective = 1000000 * sum(protection[p] for scheduled p) + 10000 * scheduled_count - 100 * setup_total - makespan - sum(wave[p] for scheduled p)` and solve by maximizing it. Output every solution as one line exactly `RESULT <wave>|<zone>|<team>|<objective>`, where MiniZinc's normal `show` representation is used for each array/value. The verifier reads the final optimal RESULT line, independently reconstructs the earliest schedule, checks every constraint and objective term, and compares the optimum on public and generated instances. Do not special-case public dimensions or values.
