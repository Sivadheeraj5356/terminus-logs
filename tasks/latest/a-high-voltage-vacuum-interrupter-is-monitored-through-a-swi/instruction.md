A high-voltage vacuum interrupter is monitored through a switching window while contact current, chamber pressure, and contact gap drift between instrument samples. Complete the C++17 numerical model in `/app/src/main.cpp` so the existing `/app/Makefile` builds `/app/arcguard`; the self-contained executable must perform the reconstruction without helper processes, read newline-delimited JSON from stdin, and print one JSON array of operating spans to stdout.

The first line is `{"window_start":T0,"window_end":T1,"strike_level":SL,"clear_level":CL,"current_weight":CW,"pressure_weight":PW,"gap_weight":GW,"current_slope":CS,"pressure_slope":PS,"gap_slope":GS,"conditioning_relief":CR,"qualify_sec":QS,"erosion_limit":EL,"start_current":SI,"start_pressure":SP,"start_gap":SG}` with `clear_level < strike_level`. Later lines are `{"t":TS,"kind":"current_sample","value":V}`, `{"t":TS,"kind":"pressure_sample","value":V}`, `{"t":TS,"kind":"gap_sample","value":V}`, `{"t":TS,"kind":"conditioning_on"}`, or `{"t":TS,"kind":"conditioning_off"}`. The three sampled quantities reset only on their matching sample and otherwise advance at their named slopes per second, including during conditioning. At the smallest remaining event time, collect all events no more than `1e-9` later, advance to that smallest time, then apply that group in original input-line order. The instantaneous dielectric stress is `current_weight*current^2 + pressure_weight*pressure^2 - gap_weight*gap - conditioning_relief` while conditioning is on, and the same expression without the final relief otherwise.

The output must cover `[window_start,window_end]` exactly using ordered contiguous objects `{"state","start","end"}`; boundaries must be accurate within `1e-7`. The states are `normal`, `restrike`, `conditioning`, and `welded`; omit zero-length spans, merge adjacent equal states, and add no zero-length tail when a boundary equals `window_end`. While not in restrike, stress at or above `strike_level` qualifies and uninterrupted qualifying time of `qualify_sec` enters restrike; any lower stress resets unfinished qualification. Restrike persists through the hysteresis band and clears only when stress reaches or falls below `clear_level`. While restrike is active, erosion accumulates the time integral of `stress-clear_level`, persists after a later clear, and latches welded on reaching `erosion_limit`. Conditioning changes the stress as above and displays `conditioning` ahead of `normal` or `restrike`, but it pauses neither drift, qualification, nor erosion; `welded` is latched and has display priority over conditioning.

Threshold equality applies at every instant, including an isolated tangent contact at a quadratic root: roots are state-checking instants, not merely interval partition points. Thus a restrike clears when a minimum touches `clear_level`, and with zero qualification a tangent touch of `strike_level` enters restrike.
