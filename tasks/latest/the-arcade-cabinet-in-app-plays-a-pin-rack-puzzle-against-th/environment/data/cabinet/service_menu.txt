SKITTLE ALLEY OPERATOR SERVICE MENU

  01 COIN AUDIT            reports coins, credits and free plays since reset
  02 LAMP TEST             cycles the marquee and the lane lamps
  03 DIAL TEST             steps the ball dial through every face and back
  04 LATCH TEST            files a throw under each latch code in turn
  05 RACK TEST             sets up and clears a service rack on each lane
  06 SERVICE LINK          opens the line link described in link_protocol.txt
  07 ATTRACT               replays the attract sequence held in the ROM
  08 HIGH SCORE RESET      clears the score card back to factory names
  09 BOOKKEEPING RESET     clears the coin audit

The service link is the only menu entry that leaves the cabinet under outside
control. It was added for the factory test rig and was never documented in the
operator manual shipped to sites.

The practice cabinet on this machine boots straight into the service link. The
tabulating engine on the board sizes its own scratch memory at power on, and
the board refuses any rack it cannot hold, which is why the service band is far
smaller than a tournament rack.
