SKITTLE ALLEY CABINET DUMP

What came off the machine when the cabinet was pulled out of storage and put on
the bench. Broadwater Amusement Co, London, 1981, boardset SKAL-0001-B.

  cabinet/    the service link the factory test rig spoke to the board over,
              the operator service menu, the printable strings that survived in
              the game ROM, the edge connector pinout, and what is known about
              the two board revisions the factory shipped
  attract/    the ten demonstration replays held in the ROM, recorded off this
              board, and an index of them
  flyer/      the operator flyer and the high score card that shipped in the
              cabinet, both of them printed for the earlier board revision and
              never reprinted
  ladder/     the notice to entrants for the tournament ladder and the link the
              tournament rig speaks to an entrant over
  tools/      a small renderer for the replay logs, and the stock entrant the
              ladder organisers hand out, which speaks the rig link end to end
              and bowls the alley badly

The board on the bench is the later revision. The paper that shipped with the
cabinet is the paper written for the earlier one, and the factory never issued a
note about what changed. The practice cabinet on this machine boots into its
service link and will set up any rack it has the memory to tabulate.
