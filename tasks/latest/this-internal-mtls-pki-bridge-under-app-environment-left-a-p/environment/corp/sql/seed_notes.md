Issue and revoke journals for the corp dump. Table issue_rows holds pid, gen, ts, scp, and role. Table revoke_rows holds pid, gen, and revoked. Multiple journals may exist; generation keys are authoritative over wall-clock ts when folding candidates. Older same-(pid,gen) issue rows may carry stale scp values that newer ts rows supersede. Revoke and handshake rows can exist for gens outside the anchor set; those must not decide windows for anchored gens.
